# Visual overhaul — what was done and how (the night of 2026-10-24)

The user's brief: the mod "looks like an old Minecraft version" next to Create; make it modern — better textures,
denser models, more blocks used in the buildings, moving machinery (the windmill), boss arenas that move, far more
weapon effects (the whip and the scythe above all), a richer fog wall. The work was stopped by the user in the morning
before the final regression; this is the record of what is on `main` (commits `796c19c` … `821cbf8`, 60 commits,
939 files, +162k / −11k lines) and how it was made.

**Nothing of it has been seen in game.** Every visual was judged only in the offline previews
(`tools/model_preview.py`). `./gradlew build`, `runGametest` and `tools/server_check.py` were **not run** on the
merged result (compile of all four source sets and the registry / template / boss JUnit are green after every merge).
The GameTests written tonight (`ArenaWallTests`, `SetPieceTests`, `BossAnimationTests`, `MobAnimationTests`,
`OverworldMobAnimationTests`) have never been executed. That regression is the first thing to do next.

## 1. How it was organised

- One coordinator session (this one) in the main checkout on `main`, three track sessions in their own worktrees
  (the user's limit of three), then two background agents for wave 2. Every track got the same art bar
  ([art-direction.md](art-direction.md)) and the rules of `COORDINATION.md` "Tracks — 0.5.0 visual overhaul": commit
  only on its branch, compile + own JUnit only, no questions, report with a MERGE REQUEST.
- The coordinator wrote the art direction first (commit `796c19c`), briefed the tracks, built its own part
  (particles, weapons, fog walls, in-hand models), merged each track with `git merge --no-ff` after checking
  authorship, attribution and `git merge-tree`, resolved the conflicts (templates, lang files, the GameTest list,
  regenerated `src/main/generated`), and kept the docs (`COORDINATION.md`, `ROADMAP.md`, `PLAN.md`, `CORE.md`).
- The usage limit stopped the sessions three times; each time they were resumed from where they stopped.
- Per-track decision logs: `plans/art-blocks.md`, `plans/bosses.md`, `plans/setpieces.md`,
  `plans/overworld-mobs.md`, `plans/mobs.md`.

## 2. The art direction (`docs/art-direction.md`, coordinator)

The rulebook every track was held to, written from what Create, Cataclysm and Mowzie's Mobs do: 16 px per block
everywhere (resolution was never the problem); one ramp of 5–7 colours per material with **hue shift** (shadows
cooler and more saturated, lights warmer and less saturated); light from the top-left with 1 px bevels; at least four
visible ramp steps and a 35 % value span per block; variation that is structural (bricks, chips, grain) instead of
per-pixel noise; big shapes first, accents on at most 5–8 % of pixels; emissive pixels on the glowmask with the base
darkened. Models: bosses 80–140 cubes / 20–40 bones, elites 50–80, ordinary mobs 30–60; corners knocked off big
forms; thin layered plates; 2–3 segment secondary bones for everything that hangs; every attack as anticipation →
strike → recovery with easing; `misc.hurt`, `misc.death`, `misc.stagger` for every mob. Places: six or more block
types per façade, decay gradients, a zone accent, motion. Effects: own particles, a signature per weapon, fog walls
as volumes.

## 3. Textures, blocks and structures (track `art-blocks`, merge `50b90b6`)

- **`tools/palette.py`** — 30 named hue-shifted ramps shared by all art (ash stone, ash brick, dead wood, charred
  wood, grave iron, gold, bone, ember, amber, moss, cloth, brass, slate, soot, straw, tar, cold flame, ember glass …),
  self-checked (`python3 tools/palette.py` verifies each ramp's hue shift), plus the drawing helpers: bevel edges,
  brick courses with variable widths, chips and cracks, wood grain with knots, cellular stone, ordered dither, a
  seam check for tiling.
- **`tools/block_art.py`** — one script writing all 72 block textures (42 redrawn, 30 new); it replaced
  `reach_world_placeholders.py` and `road_art.py` and the block part of `stele_art.py`. After the coordinator's
  review of the first pass, ash bricks became four flat-toned courses with one mortar tone and ash stone got varied
  lumps so no 16 px period shows in a 3×3. `tools/prop_art.py` was restyled onto the shared ramps (models and
  hitboxes unchanged, `PropShapesTest` green).
- **25 new building blocks** (`ModBlocks`, `ReachBlocks`, datagen models / loot / tags / recipes, names in both
  languages, render layers): ash tiles (+ cracked, stairs, slab), mossy and sooty ash bricks (+ stairs, slab, wall),
  mossy cobbled ash stone, ash cobble bricks (+ stairs, slab, wall), ash brick pillar, carved ash stone, slate tiles
  (+ stairs, slab), Sanctum rune stone (glowmask), gilded ash stone, ember glass (translucent, lit), charred planks
  (+ stairs, slab) and charred log (lit), burnt thatch, bone bricks (+ wall) and bone pillar, grave-iron grate (pane)
  and plate, rusted brass and cut brass, tar, ash cobble path, amber block (lit), crypt stone tiles (drips).
- **Every structure rebuilt** (`SanctumTemplates`, `HubTemplates`, `MinorTemplates`, `OutpostTemplates`,
  `CryptTemplates`, `CitadelTemplates`, `CityBuilder`): new masonry helpers in `TemplateBuilder` (cornice, inner
  cornice, string course, pitched roof, gable, arch, buttress, grille, blended floor, weathering, soot, weighted
  fill) and zone palettes in `Ruin` (Fields / Woods / Necropolis) and `CityBuilder` (façade, pillar, soot). Roof
  decay runs from the ridge down and never over a gap. Footprints, jigsaw names, placed enemies (115), chests and
  START_DEPTH / FOUNDATION are unchanged; the template JUnit and the preview's self-check (68 templates) are green.
  All 68 templates were rendered top / iso / eye-level and iterated on.
- Known weak spot: the Citadel pieces still read as one dark blackstone mass at eye level (the chapel and the Fields
  pieces read much better). A second pass on `CitadelTemplates` is worth it.

## 4. Own particles (coordinator, `0eb5222`)

`com.embervoid.fx.particle.EmberParticles` registers the mod's own particle types; `tools/particle_art.py` draws the
8×8 / 32×32 sprite sheets and the `particles/*.json` frame lists; `client.fx.particle` holds the factories.
Kinds: `EMBER` (rises, flickers, burns to a cinder), `ASH_FLAKE` (drifts down, settles), `SOOT` (spreads and thins),
`CINDER_SPARK` (fast, gravity, dies on the ground), `BLOOD_DROP`, `AMBER_MOTE` (twinkles), `COLD_FLAME`, `WHIP_LINE`
(a hot streak), `SOUL_WISP` (flies to a point: `SeekParticleEffect` with a target), `SHOCKWAVE` (a ring on the ground
spreading to a size), `SCORCH` (a decal lying there for a while), `BURST` (a radial flash); the last three take a
size and a colour (`TintedParticleEffect`, usable from `/particle`). Server helpers: `spawn`, `throwAll`, `line`,
`ringOut`, `shockwave`, `scorch`, `burst`, `souls`. One `DriftParticle` class with a `Kind` table serves the small
ones; `SoulWispParticle`, `DecalParticle` (a flat quad with its own geometry) and `BurstParticle` are their own.

## 5. Weapons (coordinator, `983b2a3`, `673d76d`, `b23d58c`, `d8bfb86`)

- **Warden's Scourge.** `WardensScourgeItem.crack` now returns where the lash ends (`Lash(hits, end)`): a crack
  that meets nothing still snaps in the air. The tip's report: the crack sound, a `BURST`, a `SHOCKWAVE` on the
  ground below, cinder sparks thrown out, a soot puff, a camera kick for the swinger and a shake for everyone
  within 8 blocks; every mob the lash bit sprays blood, a tip hit sprays sparks. New behaviour: a tip hit **stops the
  mob and breaks its wind-up** (`gear.weapons.whipTipStunTicks`, default 10: velocity zeroed, Slowness VI, a
  `ReachMob`'s move cancelled). Lash (the pull) draws a hot line to the mob and ash off it as it is dragged.
  Client (`ScourgeLashes`): a second additive pass draws the rope wider, dull red at the handle to a bright tip,
  growing hotter through the swing and fading after; the whole lash flashes white for the crack's instant; hot
  streaks and sparks fly off the tip while it moves.
- **`WeaponFx`** (new): a swing trail per weapon kind on every swing and every special (`CombatFx.trail`: the
  Scythe's wide ash crescent, the Rapier's thin amber sliver, the Longsword's cold sweep, the Mace's ember overhead,
  Harvest's full ring, Wide Cleave's wide fan), hooked from the hand-swing packet mixin and `SpecialAbilities`; a
  tick scheduler (`unfold`) for effects that roll over several ticks; the Huntsman's bolts tracked so they trail
  embers and burst into sparks when they go into a mob; a Scythe kill frees soul wisps that fly into the reaper
  (count by the dead's max health) with a new sound `item.ashen_scythe.reap`.
- **Ashen Scythe**: ash flakes and soot off everything the blade and the sweep cut; the stamina refund is seen as
  souls drawn out of the caught mobs; the ash wave is a front of ash and embers rolling forward over eight ticks
  that ends in a scorch; Harvest throws a ring of ash and embers with a shockwave.
- **Royal Ash Rapier**: every hit leaves a thread of amber motes from the hand to the wound, more and embers as the
  ramp climbs; the ramp's peak rings out with a burst and `item.royal_ash_rapier.ramp_peak`; a broken ramp puffs
  soot off the blade. Lunge leaves an amber streak and embers along the dash and bursts at the target.
- **Sanctum weapons**: Wide Cleave fans cold flame across its arc with a cold shockwave; Ground Slam shocks the
  ground (ring, scorch, sparks, soot) and kicks the camera (`CombatFx.impact`). Bleeding drips blood each damage tick.
- **In-hand 3D models (PLAN 17.2).** `tools/weapon_model_art.py` builds element models of the Scythe, Rapier,
  Longsword, Mace and the Scourge's handle on the icon's diagonal (parts authored up from the grip, each element
  turned −45° about Z around the grip; blade segments carry their own 22.5° steps so the scythe curves), each with
  a 64×64 sheet painted per material (grain, lit steel edges, bevelled bronze, wrapped leather). Because they sit
  where the icon's grip sits, vanilla's handheld display transforms hold them right. `client.reach.gear.InHandModels`
  swaps them in for the first- and third-person hands through the existing item-renderer mixin; the icons stay in
  the GUI, on the ground and in frames. The lash now leaves the handle's tip ring (`ScourgeLashes.ROOT`).

## 6. Fog walls (coordinator, in `673d76d`; `afad947`)

`boss.ArenaWalls` tells every player within 160 blocks of a running fight where its wall stands (centre, radius,
height, colour) every second and sends one removal when the fight ends (`ArenaWallTests`). `client.boss.FogWallDraw`
draws a wall from segments between columns: two scrolling layers of ember cloud (`textures/misc/fog_wall.png`, a
tiling turbulence sheet from `particle_art.py`, emissive, rising at different speeds), additive streaks of light
travelling along the wall, a glowing ring on the ground, embers streaming up and ash drifting off it, random flares
(a burst, sparks, `boss.fog_wall.flare`) and a hum (`boss.fog_wall.hum`, a moving sound that follows the nearest
point and swells as the player nears). `ArenaWallClient` builds the arena ring (72 segments, on the ground, fading in
and out with the fight); `FogCurtain` (the Citadel border) now uses the same drawing for its border pieces.
`BossFight` no longer sprinkles white ash along the wall; its hold on players is unchanged.

## 7. Set pieces and arenas that move (track `setpieces`, merge `efd9d3c`)

- **Framework** (`block.setpiece`, `client.setpiece`): one block entity type `embervoid:set_piece`, the block
  carrying its `SetPieceKind` (shape, animations, reactions); mode ALWAYS (loops client-side, no server ticking) or
  TRIGGERED (a synced state such as `open` / `closed` / `lit` / `spinning` and one-shot actions via GeckoLib
  triggered animations); facing, waterlogging, a hitbox per kind, distance culling, `rendersOutsideBoundingBox`;
  a per-chunk cap `setpieces.maxPerChunk` (8, enforced when a chunk's pieces load; player placement refused when
  full) and `setpieces.animate`. API `SetPieces.trigger(world, pos, "closed+slam" | "spinning 160" | "lit" | …)`.
  **Arena events**: `BossFight.fireArenaEvent` → `ArenaEvents.fire` reaches every piece within the arena radius + 8
  (`summon`, `phase <n>`, `hazard <id>` with a new default `ArenaHazard.id()`, `bossHit heavy`, `kill`, `reset`; the
  Keeper fires `raise_dead`, a Grave Warden's ring tolls great bells in range). Sound keyframes carry a word the
  client maps to vanilla sounds.
- **Fourteen pieces** by `tools/setpiece_art.py` (geo / animations / textures under `*/block/setpiece/`): windmill
  sails (+ torn), granary hoist, hanging scales, ash mill, hanging cage, great gear, great bell, sluice grate, great
  brazier (stands one block above a campfire and follows its lit state, so the bosses' campfire logic is untouched),
  portcullis, forge bellows, chain winch, censer.
- **Arenas**: Fields — the ash mill spins up in the Colossus's storm, scales sway under the stalls; Woods — cages on
  the wall, a gallows wheel turning from phase 1; Necropolis — a great bell in a niche tolls on `raise_dead` and each
  phase, sluice grates open when the flood rises; Citadel — the portcullis slams at the summon and opens at the kill,
  great gears turn through the fight, braziers ignite per phase; Sanctum — censers ignite with the braziers.
- **Placement**: the windmill's fence frame replaced by the sails, hoists in barns and the granary, bells in the
  chapel and mausoleum, cages at the gallows and the hanging cages, the gatehouse portcullis, bellows and winches in
  the Citadel, censers in the dungeon chapel and the hub, lit braziers on the toll gates.

## 8. Bosses (track `bosses`, merge `c1c19d5`)

Cubes / bones: Ash Colossus 108 / 26 (was 24 / 9), Smoldering Lord 105 / 28, Crypt Keeper 103 / 27, Grieving Tree
128 / 35, Fallen Sister 78 / 25, Vampire boss 71 / 24. The art kit gained `chamfered` (a big form as three boxes with

crown), capes / chains / straps / hair / cords that sway. Every move re-keyed anticipation → strike → recovery with
the wind-up ticks unchanged (the 18.4 balance; `EntityModelFilesTest` checks animation length ≥ wind-up).
Code: `ReachBoss` has a `hurt` controller (a flinch never over a wind-up, 10-tick cooldown), `misc.stagger` where a
state exists, `misc.death` held on the last frame with the body kept `deathTicks()` and no vanilla death tilt, phase
animations under their old names; the same in `VampireBossEntity`. `CombatFx` and the own particles on every slam,
landing, sweep, shatter, phase change and death (43 stand-ins replaced; souls rise out of a dying Keeper, Tree and
Sister). `EmberHaloLayer` (the glowmask drawn again, scaled, translucent, pulsing) on the Lord and the Colossus's
bare core. `BossAnimationTests` written (hurt never cancels a wind-up; bodies stay for the death animation).
