## Where to find it

Ashen Zombies never spawn on their own. They come on the **full moon** (see
[Moon nights](../../../guides/mechanics/moon-nights.md)) for players who carry the **Curse of the Night**: one zombie for
each stack of the curse, up to ten <!-- nightMobs.maxZombies -->, appearing 16 to 32 blocks away
<!-- nightMobs.spawnMinDistance, nightMobs.spawnMaxDistance -->. You get a curse stack each time a hostile mob (or a
boss) kills you; the stacks reset when a full moon ends. A player with no curse gets [Ghosts](ghost.md) instead.

## Behaviour

A sturdier zombie, grey with ash. It walks at you and hits like a heavy zombie. It is five levels above what an
ordinary mob would be at that spot <!-- nightMobs.zombieLevelBonus -->, so it hurts more than the zombies around it.

The sun does not burn it: a wave still standing at dawn keeps walking.

There is nothing tricky about a single one; the danger is a whole wave after many deaths. Fight from a doorway or a
pillar so they come one or two at a time, or thin them with a bow as they walk in.

Your wave holds the night: dawn does not come until every zombie of the wave is dead. Any left when the night ends
another way vanish.
