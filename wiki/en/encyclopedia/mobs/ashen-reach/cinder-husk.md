## Where to find it

The Cinder Husk is the common foot soldier of the [Ashen Fields](../../places/ashen-fields.md). It spawns naturally all
over the zone in small groups, wherever the block light is low: a torched camp stays clear. More stand in the Fields'
ruins (farmsteads, barns, pilgrim camps, the gallows) and at the zone's road outposts. In a party, the
[Ash Colossus](../../bosses/ash-colossus.md) calls extra Husks into its arena for every fighter beyond the first.

## Behaviour

A burnt peasant with a pitchfork, slow and plain. It walks straight at you and, once in reach, raises its arm with a
rasping wind-up before it strikes. The wind-up is short but always the same, so learn the rhythm: step back as the arm
goes up, then hit it while it recovers. It never blocks or dodges, so a Husk on its own is no threat.

The danger is numbers. Groups come in threes and fours and gather on the roads, so do not let them surround you. Pull
them into a line, or fight with your back to a wall, and take them one swing at a time. Embers drift off a living Husk,
which makes it easy to spot in the ash.

On top of the table, a Husk can drop a {{item embervoid:cinder_shard}}, and a Husk killed by a player can drop a piece
