## Where to find it

The Ash Stalker hunts the [Ashen Fields](../../places/ashen-fields.md). It spawns alone, and more lie in wait in the
scarecrow rows and the farmsteads.

## Behaviour

An ambusher. A Stalker lies buried in a furrow, invisible and untouchable: you cannot hit it, push it or see it. When
you come within a few blocks <!-- AshStalkerEntity.TRIGGER_RANGE --> the ash at that spot starts to boil, a hiss goes
up and the Stalker rises out of the ground for a second <!-- AshStalkerEntity.BOIL_TICKS -->. It is still invulnerable
while it rises. Then it leaps at you and claws you if the leap connects.

That hiss is your warning. Step back or sideways the moment you hear it, so the leap lands short, then fight it in the
open: out of the ground it has a quick clawing swing with a short wind-up and nothing else. Left without a target for a
while, it digs itself in again.

Walk the furrows carefully and listen. The {{item embervoid:scarecrow_mask}} hides you from buried Stalkers
completely: wearing it, you can walk right past them.
