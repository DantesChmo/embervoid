## Where to find it

The Smoldering Knight is the elite of the [Smoldering Citadel](../../places/smoldering-citadel.md). Knights are placed
only, in the towers and the keep, and stay dead once killed.

## Behaviour

The king's guard with a greatsword: heavily armored and immune to fire.

- **Three-hit combo.** In reach it swings three blows in a row: two quick side sweeps, then a heavy overhead blow that
  strikes the ground in front of it. The wind-ups are short, and every blow lands even right after the one before, so
  your hit immunity does not save you. Do not trade blows: back out of reach as the first sweep starts, or block with a
  shield, and step in after the overhead finisher.
- **Lunge.** When you are a few blocks away <!-- SmolderingKnightEntity.LUNGE_MIN, LUNGE_MAX --> it may crouch low for
  three quarters of a second <!-- SmolderingKnightEntity.CROUCH_TICKS -->, then dash about six blocks straight at you and
  run you through, harder than a combo blow. The crouch is your cue: sidestep the line of the dash.

The safest window is right after the combo's heavy finisher or a missed lunge. Do not hang around at mid range, where it
lunges; stay either out of its sight or right next to it.

## Drops

The Knight is the Citadel's elite. A player kill can drop the {{item embervoid:furnace_heart_cuirass}} at the elite
rate and, rarely, one of the Citadel's accessories: the {{item embervoid:seal_of_the_ashen_king}} or the
{{item embervoid:ring_of_embers}}.
