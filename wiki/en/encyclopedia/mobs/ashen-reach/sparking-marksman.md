## Where to find it

The Sparking Marksman guards the [Smoldering Citadel](../../places/smoldering-citadel.md). It is placed only: on the
gatehouse roof and along the city walls. Killed, it does not come back. The
[Smoldering Lord](../../bosses/smoldering-lord.md) brings more of them onto the keep wall when his fight starts.

## Behaviour

A crossbowman who never leaves its post. It does not walk and cannot be knocked back; it only turns to face you and
shoots whenever it has a clear line within a long range. <!-- SparkingMarksmanEntity.RANGE -->

Each shot is telegraphed: a spark lights on the bolt tip and glows brighter for a full second
<!-- SparkingMarksmanEntity.AIM_TICKS --> before the bolt flies. The bolt is fast, hits hard and sets you on fire.

When you see the spark, break its line of sight: step behind a merlon, a wall or a house, and step out again after the
shot. A shield blocks the bolt too. Crossing open ground under a wall with several Marksmen is the dangerous part of
the Citadel, so move from cover to cover and time each dash just after a shot.

Since it cannot move, a Marksman is easy prey once you reach it. Climb up to it, or shoot back between its shots, and
take the wall's Marksmen out one at a time before you cross the yard below.
