## Where to find it

The Ashen Carrion is the elite of the [Ashen Fields](../../places/ashen-fields.md). It is rare as a natural spawn;
more often you find one perched on a windmill, a scarecrow row, the granary vault or a road outpost.

## Behaviour

A big carrion bird. Perched, it sits still; once it has a target it takes off and circles above you, out of sword reach.
Every so often, and at once when you drop below half health, it dives. Each dive is telegraphed: the bird shrieks and
hangs in the air while a dark shadow gathers at your feet, tightening for a moment <!-- AshenCarrionEntity.SHRIEK_TICKS -->
before it comes down. The dive hits once on contact, then the bird climbs back to its circle.

When you hear the shriek and see the shadow, sidestep out of it or turn and meet the bird: it flies straight at you and
is in melee reach for an instant. A bow or crossbow works best, since it spends most of the fight circling in the open.
Hitting a perched Carrion wakes it at once.

Keep your health up: a wounded player draws dive after dive.
