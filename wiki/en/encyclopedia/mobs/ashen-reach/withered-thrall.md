## Where to find it

The Withered Thrall is the common enemy of the [Royal Woods](../../places/royal-woods.md). It spawns naturally across
the zone, alone or in pairs, in low block light. More are placed in the Woods' ruins: the kennels, the park wall, the
hunting lodge, the road outposts, and the hanging cages, where they wait above the path. The
[Grieving Tree](../../bosses/grieving-tree.md) drops Thralls into its arena during the fight.

## Behaviour

A hanged poacher. On the ground it walks up to you and claws, raising its hands in a short wind-up first; step back as
the hands come up and strike after the blow.

The caged ones are the trap. A Thrall hanging in a cage drops on you when you pass underneath: first the chain rattles
for half a second <!-- WitheredThrallEntity.RATTLE_TICKS -->, then it lets go, opening the cage's trapdoor if there is
one, and hits you if it lands on you. When you hear the rattle, keep moving and get out from under it. Better still,
look up as you walk under cages: a hit or an arrow knocks a hanging Thrall off its chain at once, on your terms, and
it takes no fall damage from its own drop.

Alone a Thrall is weak. In the Woods, though, it rarely comes alone: a Lantern Hag behind it is the usual pairing.
