## What it does

The way out of a realm. Right-click it to go back to the [Ember Sanctum](../../places/ember-sanctum.md). It does
nothing outside a realm.

## Where to find it

On the north side of the [Crossroads](../../places/crossroads.md), the landing plaza of
[Ashen Reach](../../places/ashen-reach.md). It cannot be broken, crafted or moved.
