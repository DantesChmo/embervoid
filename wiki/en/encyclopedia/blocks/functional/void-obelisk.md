## What it does

The way into a realm. Each obelisk leads to one realm, and each player sees it in one of three states, from their own
progress:

- **Dark:** the flame has not reached that realm yet. Nothing happens.
- **Lit:** the realm is open to you. Right-click to travel there: to the last shrine you rested at in that realm, or to
  its landing if you have not rested there yet. Sneak while you click to go to the landing anyway.
- **Kindled:** you completed the realm. It still takes you there.

[Ashen Reach](../../places/ashen-reach.md) opens on your first visit to the hub. Beating the
[Smoldering Lord](../../bosses/smoldering-lord.md) completes it and lights the next obelisk, though that realm is still
forming in 0.5.3.

## Where to find it

A ring of four stands in the [Ember Sanctum](../../places/ember-sanctum.md). Sanctum cores of worlds made before 0.5.0
keep theirs too. It cannot be broken, crafted or moved.
