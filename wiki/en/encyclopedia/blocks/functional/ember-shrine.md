## What it does

The rest point. Right-click a shrine to rest: your health and your [Healing Flask](../../items/consumables/healing-flask.md)
fill up. In a realm, the shrine you rested at last is also where you come back after dying, and where the
[Void Obelisk](void-obelisk.md) puts you when you return to that realm. Resting in the hub or a dungeon does not move
your respawn point.

Use a {{item embervoid:flask_shard}} on a shrine to offer it: your flask gains a charge. Use
{{item embervoid:ash_of_rematch}} on an arena's shrine to call its beaten boss back.

The shrine outside each boss arena of [Ashen Reach](../../places/ashen-reach.md) stays dormant for you, cold to your
eyes, until you have beaten that boss ("The flame does not answer you"). After the kill it kindles and works like any
other. More in [Ember Shrines and respawn](../../../guides/mechanics/ember-shrines.md).

## Where to find it

In the [Ember Sanctum](../../places/ember-sanctum.md) hub, at the boss door of every
[Ruined Sanctum](../../places/ruined-sanctum.md), at the realm's [Crossroads](../../places/crossroads.md) landing,
outside every boss arena, and in the [Smoldering Citadel](../../places/smoldering-citadel.md). It cannot be broken,
crafted or moved.
