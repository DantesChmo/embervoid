## What it does

A lantern with a live ember in it. Placed, it hangs or stands like a vanilla lantern and gives a little less light
than a vanilla lantern. Carried in your offhand and fed {{item embervoid:amber_lantern_oil}}, it burns for 10 minutes
<!-- gear.consumables.oilMinutes --> and lets you see in the dark, as with Night Vision: the light you want in the
crypts of the [Sunken Necropolis](../../places/sunken-necropolis.md).

## Where to find it

Ember Lanterns light the realm's roads and landings at dusk. Craft your own from iron nuggets, a torch and a coal or
charcoal.
