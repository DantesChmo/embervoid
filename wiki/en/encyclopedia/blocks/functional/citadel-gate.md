## What it does

A wall of ember fog that bars the road to the [Smoldering Citadel](../../places/smoldering-citadel.md). It opens for
each player on their own: once you have beaten all three zone bosses (the
[Ash Colossus](../../bosses/ash-colossus.md), the [Grieving Tree](../../bosses/grieving-tree.md) and the
[Crypt Keeper](../../bosses/crypt-keeper.md)), you walk straight through it. Until then it is solid to you, and to
anything that is not a player. Right-click it to ask: it tells you it yields, or how many guardians still stand.

Ender pearls, chorus fruit, elytra and Riptide do not get you past it early.

## Where to find it

On the south rim of the [Crossroads](../../places/crossroads.md), the landing plaza of
[Ashen Reach](../../places/ashen-reach.md), where the gate road leaves for the Citadel. It cannot be broken, crafted or
moved.
