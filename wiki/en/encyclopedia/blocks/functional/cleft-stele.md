## What it does

Your own gate to the [Ember Sanctum](../../places/ember-sanctum.md). Build a ritual circle of
{{item embervoid:cleft_stone}}: a centre stone and four pillars of 3 Cleft Stone, two blocks out from it on each side,
13 stones in all, with the 3 blocks above the centre left open. Use a {{item embervoid:crying_soul}} on any of its
stones: the soul is spent, the pillars flare and a column of mist, the **Stele Rift**, rises over the centre. Walk
into it, or right-click a lit stone, to reach the hub; leaving the hub by its own stele brings you back beside it.

Only one stele burns per world, besides the hub's own. Kindling a new one puts the old one out, and so does breaking
a stone of a lit one. Steles burn in the Overworld, the Nether and the End, not in a realm or the hub.

Before 0.5.3 the stele was a flat slab, 5 wide and 6 tall, with a crack, the **Stele Crack**, down its middle column.
Slabs built or lit before still work. The crack and the rift have no item: they appear when a stele is kindled and go
when it is put out. See [Kindling the Cleft Stele](../../../guides/walkthrough/cleft-stele.md).

## Where to find it

Every [Ruined Sanctum](../../places/ruined-sanctum.md) keeps a cold circle on its arena's dais, behind the
[Fallen Sister](../../bosses/fallen-sister.md). To build one anywhere else, craft Cleft Stone from 8 stone bricks
around a coal or charcoal.
