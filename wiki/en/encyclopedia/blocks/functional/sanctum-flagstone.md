## What it does

The floor of a Sanctum core. Nothing hostile spawns on it, and the Vampires of a
[blood new moon](../../../guides/mechanics/moon-nights.md) do not come for a player standing on it in the Overworld.
It cannot be broken or blown up.

## Where to find it

Under the shrine and the core of the [Ember Sanctum](../../places/ember-sanctum.md), and in the cores of Sanctums from
worlds made before 0.5.0. It cannot be crafted.
