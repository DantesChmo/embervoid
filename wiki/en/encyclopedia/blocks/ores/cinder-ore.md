## Where to find it

In the ash stone under the [Ashen Fields](../../places/ashen-fields.md), and only there. It is common, about as common
as iron, from near the bottom of the world up to y 80, most of it around the middle of that range
<!-- placed_feature ashen_reach/cinder_ore: trapezoid y 4–80 -->. Mine it with an iron pickaxe or better. It glows
faintly, which helps in caves.

## Drops

{{drops}}

1 to 3 {{item embervoid:cinder_shard}} and a little XP; Fortune adds more shards, Silk Touch gives the ore block. The
{{item embervoid:delvers_ring}} mines it faster.
