## Where to find it

In the ash stone under the [Royal Woods](../../places/royal-woods.md), and only there, between y 30 and y 90
<!-- placed_feature ashen_reach/amber_vein: uniform y 30–90 -->: look in ravine walls and under roots. It is rarer than
Cinder Ore, about as common as gold. Mine it with an iron pickaxe or better.

{{item embervoid:delvers_ring}} mines it faster.
