## Where to find it

Deep in the ash stone under the [Sunken Necropolis](../../places/sunken-necropolis.md), and only there, from near the
bottom of the world up to y 44 <!-- placed_feature ashen_reach/grave_iron_ore: uniform y 2–44 -->: the sinkhole walls
and the crypts cut into them. It is the rarest of the three, about as common as diamond, and seldom shows on an open
face. Mine it with a diamond pickaxe or better.

## Drops

One {{item embervoid:raw_grave_iron}}, no XP; Fortune adds more, Silk Touch gives the ore block. Smelt the raw iron
into a {{item embervoid:grave_iron_ingot}}. The {{item embervoid:delvers_ring}} mines it faster.
