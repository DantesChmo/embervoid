The stone of [Ashen Reach](../../places/ashen-reach.md): its ground is ash stone wherever you dig, and the cut, polished, carved and gilded kinds build the [Ruined Sanctum](../../places/ruined-sanctum.md), the [Ember Sanctum](../../places/ember-sanctum.md) and the realm's ruins. Smelt cobbled ash stone back into ash stone. Gilded ash stone trims the [Smoldering Citadel](../../places/smoldering-citadel.md); the glowing Sanctum rune stone marks the Sanctums.
