Scenery for the realm's ruins, farms, crypts and the [Smoldering Citadel](../../places/smoldering-citadel.md): grain
sacks and broken carts in the [Ashen Fields](../../places/ashen-fields.md), coffins, grave crosses and bone piles in the
[Sunken Necropolis](../../places/sunken-necropolis.md), weapon racks, cages and standards in the outposts and the city.
The props are static models. The set pieces move: windmill sails turn, the granary hoist and the hanging scales sway,
the great gear and the chain winch grind, the great brazier and the censer burn. In a boss arena they react to the
fight, so a portcullis slams shut, a bell tolls or a brazier flares as the boss rises, changes phase or falls.

None of them do anything for you; they drop themselves when broken, so you can carry them home as decoration. A chunk
holds a limited number of set pieces <!-- setpieces.maxPerChunk 8 -->; place more and the game tells you there is too
much machinery there already.
