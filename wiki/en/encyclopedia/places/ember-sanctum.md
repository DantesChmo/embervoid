## How to get there

The Ember Sanctum is the hub: one small island floating in a void under an eternal dusk, in its own dimension. All
players share it.

The way in from the overworld is a lit [Cleft Stele](../blocks/functional/cleft-stele.md). Kindle one with the
{{item embervoid:crying_soul}} that the [Fallen Sister](../bosses/fallen-sister.md) drops in a
[Ruined Sanctum](ruined-sanctum.md): the cold stele in her arena, or one you build from Cleft Stone anywhere in the
overworld, the Nether or the End. Walk into its column of mist (or right-click it) and you arrive on the hub's landing.
Only one stele per world burns at a time; lighting a new one puts the old one out.

Leaving a realm, by its Return Obelisk or after its final boss, also brings you here.

Your first arrival unlocks the first realm, [Ashen Reach](ashen-reach.md).

## What is there

- **The landing** on the north edge of the island, where you arrive, and behind it the hub's own Cleft Stele, always
  lit. It is the way out: it takes you to the world's lit stele. With none lit, it takes you back to where you last
  left the overworld, or failing that to your bed or the world spawn.
- **The [Ember Shrine](../blocks/functional/ember-shrine.md)** in the middle of the open core. Resting heals you fully
  and refills your [Healing Flask](../items/consumables/healing-flask.md); use a {{item embervoid:flask_shard}} on it to
  add a charge. Resting here does not set your respawn point.
- **The [Ashen Maiden](../npcs/ashen-maiden.md)**, just south of the shrine. Talk to her to spend experience on stats.
- **The [Void Obelisks](../blocks/functional/void-obelisk.md)**, a ring of four around the shrine, one per realm. Each
  shows your own progress: dark (not yet reached), lit (unlocked) or kindled (completed). Right-click a lit obelisk to
  travel to its realm, to the last shrine you rested at there; sneak and right-click to go to the realm's landing
  instead. Only the first realm exists yet; beating the [Smoldering Lord](../bosses/smoldering-lord.md) lights the
  second obelisk, which leads nowhere for now.

The hub is safe: no hostile mobs spawn, and nothing on the island can be broken or built outside creative mode. The
edge is not safe. A fall into the void kills you, and you respawn at your overworld bed or the world spawn.
