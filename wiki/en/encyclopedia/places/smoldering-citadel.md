## How to find it

The Smoldering Citadel is the fourth sector of [Ashen Reach](ashen-reach.md), the narrow slice south of the
[Crossroads](crossroads.md): the kingdom's burning capital. A burnt city wall runs along both its borders, and you can
see the city glow over it from the open zones.

The only way in is the [Citadel Gate](../blocks/functional/citadel-gate.md) on the south side of the plaza. It opens for
you once you have beaten all three zone guardians: the [Ash Colossus](../bosses/ash-colossus.md), the
[Grieving Tree](../bosses/grieving-tree.md) and the [Crypt Keeper](../bosses/crypt-keeper.md). Until then the sector is
closed to you in every way: climbing the wall, digging under it, ender pearls, elytra or a boat all put you back
outside. Each player's way in depends only on their own progress.

Walk through the gate and down the gate road. Where it ends stands the first Citadel
[Ember Shrine](../blocks/functional/ember-shrine.md): rest there. From then on, dying in the Citadel brings you back to
the last shrine you rested at inside it, or to this one.

## What is there

{{mobs-in}}

A dark red sky and red fog, cracks of glowing embers in the ground and smouldering logs and debris everywhere. Mob
levels run from 25 to 30 <!-- Realms.ASHEN_REACH final band -->, the highest in the realm.

**The Burning City.** The city starts about a hundred blocks past the gate. The road leads you through it in order:

1. **the outskirts**, the edge of the city;
2. **the burning street** and **the market**;
3. **the gatehouse** in the city wall on the rim of the Citadel hill, with **towers** at both ends of the wall;
4. **the inner ring**, two long wings around the courtyard;
5. **the courtyard** below the keep wall, the Smoldering Lord's arena, about 300 blocks from the landing;
6. **the keep** behind it: a forecourt, the great hall with the king's empty throne, and the treasury.

**Mobs.** [Cinder Hounds](../mobs/ashen-reach/cinder-hound.md) roam the city in packs; they are its only natural
spawn. Everything else is placed and never comes back once killed:
[Sparking Marksmen](../mobs/ashen-reach/sparking-marksman.md) on the gatehouse roof and the walls,
[Furnace Golems](../mobs/ashen-reach/furnace-golem.md) in the inner ring, and
[Smoldering Knights](../mobs/ashen-reach/smoldering-knight.md), the elite, in the towers and the keep.

**Loot.** Chests along the way hold food, iron, gold, {{item embervoid:ash_bomb}}s, {{item embervoid:amber_balm}},
all three zone materials, {{item embervoid:smoldering_resin}} and {{item embervoid:amber_lantern_oil}}. The treasury in
the keep holds the realm's best chest: diamonds, {{item embervoid:smoldering_ingot}}s and the
{{item embervoid:smoldering_template}}, pieces of all three zone armor sets, the Citadel's Reliquary accessories and,
rarely, the {{item embervoid:royal_ash_rapier}}. Golems and Knights can drop the
{{item embervoid:furnace_heart_cuirass}}. The Citadel has no ore and no armor set of its own.

## The boss

In the courtyard below the keep wall waits the [Smoldering Lord](../bosses/smoldering-lord.md), first up on the wall,
then down among you once you light the three signal braziers. An Ember Shrine outside the courtyard's fog wall wakes
once you have beaten him. Beating him completes Ashen Reach.
