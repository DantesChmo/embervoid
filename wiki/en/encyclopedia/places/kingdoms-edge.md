## How to find it

The Kingdom's Edge is the rim of [Ashen Reach](ashen-reach.md). From the [Crossroads](crossroads.md), walk straight out
in any direction, past the arenas and through whichever zone you are in, for about 1200 blocks
<!-- Realms.ASHEN_REACH radius 1300; EdgeFeature.WALL_INSET -->. There is no road to it and nothing you need there; it is
where the kingdom ends.

## What is there

{{mobs-in}}

- **The border wall.** About a hundred blocks before the end of the land <!-- EdgeFeature.WALL_INSET --> a broken
  wall with towers runs all the way round the realm, through every zone and the Citadel's sector too.
- **The fallen bridges.** Six bridges <!-- EdgeFeature.BRIDGES --> run out from the cliffs of the open zones over the
  abyss and break off in mid-air. They once led out of the kingdom; now they lead nowhere.
- **The abyss.** Past the cliffs there is no ground at all, only a dark fog over the void. A fall is a void death: you
  respawn at your last rested shrine and leave an [Ash Remnant](../../guides/mechanics/death-and-remnant.md) on the last
  solid ground you stood on, never in the void. If you somehow get far out over the abyss, the void pushes you back
  <!-- RealmEvents.VOID_MARGIN -->.

There are no shrines or roads at the edge. Mind your step on the bridges: their ends are broken.
