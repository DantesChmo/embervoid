## How to find it

The Sunken Necropolis is the [Ashen Reach](ashen-reach.md) zone east of the [Crossroads](crossroads.md). Take the east
road out of the plaza. It is the hardest of the three open zones, mob levels 21 to 25
<!-- Realms.ASHEN_REACH level band zone_c -->.

## What is there

{{mobs-in}}

The kingdom's graveyard, sunk into a basin full of sinkholes, under a blue-grey fog. Gravel, tuff and cracked ash
bricks, candles burning on the graves, lone gravestones. It is the only zone with water: pools lie at the bottom of the
sinkholes, and the crypts below are flooded.

**Mobs.** [Restless Bones](../mobs/ashen-reach/restless-bones.md) are everywhere, and
[Crypt Moths](../mobs/ashen-reach/crypt-moth.md) flutter under roofs, drawn to bright light. Placed in the ruins and
crypts you meet the [Hollow Sentinel](../mobs/ashen-reach/hollow-sentinel.md), a shield-bearing guard, and the
[Grave Warden](../mobs/ashen-reach/grave-warden.md), the zone's elite, who raises the dead. Placed mobs never come
back once killed.

**Ruins.** Small and large mausoleums, rows of graves, chapels, ossuary pits and collapsed tombs. Most have a chest of
food, iron, gold, {{item embervoid:ash_bomb}}s, {{item embervoid:amber_balm}}, {{item embervoid:raw_grave_iron}} and
{{item embervoid:amber_lantern_oil}}.

**The crypts.** Crypt entrances lead down into a chain of underground rooms: a descent, junctions and corridors, a
flooded hall, burial halls, a warden's chamber and, at the bottom, the lower crypt, a flooded vault. The zone's
**treasure** waits on a dais there: it can hold diamonds, Smoldering materials, pieces of the
[Warden's Set](../items/armor/wardens-set.md), the Necropolis' Reliquary accessories and, rarely, the
{{item embervoid:wardens_scourge}}. The crypts are dark: bring torches, or an {{item embervoid:ember_lantern}} with
Amber Lantern Oil in your offhand.

**Ore.** [Grave Iron Ore](../blocks/ores/grave-iron-ore.md) is the zone's own; it gives Raw Grave Iron.

**The road.** Two outposts stand on the road, a toll gate and a watchtower, each with Bones and Moths.

## The boss

At the road's end, a deep sinkhole with a switchback stair down into a flooded ossuary is the arena of the
[Crypt Keeper](../bosses/crypt-keeper.md). Bring flint and steel. An
[Ember Shrine](../blocks/functional/ember-shrine.md) and a chest wait on the rim outside its fog wall; the shrine wakes
once you have beaten the Keeper. The chest can hold {{item embervoid:ash_of_rematch}}, a
{{item embervoid:smoldering_template}}, Amber Lantern Oil, Amber Balm and Raw Grave Iron.
