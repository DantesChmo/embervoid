## How to find it

The Crossroads is where you arrive in [Ashen Reach](ashen-reach.md): the kingdom's old crossroads plaza at the very
centre of the realm. Sneak and right-click the realm's lit [Void Obelisk](../blocks/functional/void-obelisk.md) in the
[Ember Sanctum](ember-sanctum.md) to land here. A plain right-click takes you to the last shrine you rested at in the
realm instead, and to the Crossroads only if you have not rested anywhere yet.

When you die in the realm and have not rested at any other shrine, you come back here.

## What is there

{{mobs-in}}

A round brick plaza with a low, broken wall on its rim. The plaza is protected: you cannot build or break anything on
it.

- **The [Ember Shrine](../blocks/functional/ember-shrine.md)** in the middle, on a disc of polished stone. Rest here to
  heal, refill your flask and set your respawn point.
- **The [Return Obelisk](../blocks/functional/return-obelisk.md)** on the north side. It takes you back to the hub.
- **The [Citadel Gate](../blocks/functional/citadel-gate.md)** on the south side, a fog wall between two pillars under
  a lintel. It stays shut until you have beaten all three zone guardians; then it lets you through onto the gate road
  to the [Smoldering Citadel](smoldering-citadel.md).
- **The roads.** Gaps in the rim wall, each flanked by two lantern posts, open onto three ash-brick roads: west to the
  [Ashen Fields](ashen-fields.md), north to the [Royal Woods](royal-woods.md) and east to the
  [Sunken Necropolis](sunken-necropolis.md). Each road ends at its zone's boss arena. Milestones along it count the
  way, and two ruined outposts on each road, a toll gate and a watchtower, hold enemies of that zone.

Lost? Hold an {{item embervoid:ember_lodestar}}: in the realm its needle points to the nearest arena you have not beaten.
