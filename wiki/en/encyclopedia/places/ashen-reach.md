Ashen Reach is the first realm: one burnt kingdom under eternal dusk, in its own dimension. You enter it from its
[Void Obelisk](../blocks/functional/void-obelisk.md) in the [Ember Sanctum](ember-sanctum.md). It unlocks the first
time you reach the hub.

Nothing burns in daylight here, because there is none. No vanilla or overworld mobs live here, only the kingdom's own
dead.

## The layout

The kingdom is a great disc that reaches about 1300 blocks from its centre <!-- Realms.ASHEN_REACH radius -->.
At its middle lies the [Crossroads](crossroads.md), the plaza where you land. The rest is cut into four sectors like
slices of a pie:

| Sector | Direction from the Crossroads | Mob levels <!-- Realms.ASHEN_REACH level bands --> | Guardian |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Ashen Fields](ashen-fields.md) | west | 15–18 | [Ash Colossus](../bosses/ash-colossus.md) |
| [Royal Woods](royal-woods.md) | north | 18–21 | [Grieving Tree](../bosses/grieving-tree.md) |
| [Sunken Necropolis](sunken-necropolis.md) | east | 21–25 | [Crypt Keeper](../bosses/crypt-keeper.md) |
| [Smoldering Citadel](smoldering-citadel.md) | south | 25–30 | [Smoldering Lord](../bosses/smoldering-lord.md) |

The three open zones share the rest of the circle evenly; their borders wander. The Citadel's sector is narrower and walled off
by a burnt city wall along both its borders. Past all of them, at the rim, is the
[Kingdom's Edge](kingdoms-edge.md): a broken border wall, cliffs and the abyss.

Each zone has its own fog, mobs, ruins and ore. Mob levels rise from the Fields to the Woods to the Necropolis.

## The zones in any order

The three open zones are all open from the start. Take them in any order you like. The Fields are the gentlest, the
Necropolis the hardest. Each zone's guardian waits in an arena
at the end of that zone's road. The guardians' levels are fixed, so you can come back stronger to a zone you left.

An ash-brick road runs from the Crossroads to each arena, with a milestone every hundred blocks and two ruined
outposts on the way. Off the roads lie farms, lodges, mausoleums and other ruins with placed enemies and chests. The
{{item embervoid:ember_lodestar}} points to the nearest arena you have not beaten.

## The Citadel Gate

On the south side of the Crossroads stands the [Citadel Gate](../blocks/functional/citadel-gate.md), a wall of ember
fog. It stays shut for you until you have beaten all three zone guardians; right-click it and it tells you how many
still stand. Then you walk through it and down the gate road into the burning capital, the Smoldering Citadel, and
its master, the Smoldering Lord. Nothing gets you into that sector early: not climbing the wall, not tunnelling, not
pearls or elytra. Each player's gate opens on their own progress.

Beating the Smoldering Lord completes the realm.

## Shrines, death and leaving

Rest at an [Ember Shrine](../blocks/functional/ember-shrine.md) to heal, refill your flask and set your respawn
point. When you die in Ashen Reach you come back at the last shrine you rested at in the realm, or at the Crossroads.
The Citadel's sector keeps its own: dying there brings you back to the last shrine you rested at inside it, or to the
first Citadel shrine at the end of the gate road. Your bed in the overworld is untouched, and beds here do not set
your spawn.

Outside the Crossroads and the arenas you can build and mine. To leave, use the Return Obelisk at the Crossroads; it
takes you to the hub.
