## How to find it

The Ashen Fields are the [Ashen Reach](ashen-reach.md) zone west of the [Crossroads](crossroads.md). Take the west road
out of the plaza. They are the gentlest of the three open zones, mob levels 15 to 18
<!-- Realms.ASHEN_REACH level band zone_a -->, and a good first stop.

## What is there

{{mobs-in}}

The kingdom's burnt farmland: a low, rolling plain of ash soil and burnt furrows under a grey-ochre fog, with dead
bushes and piles of burnt straw. There is no water anywhere.

**Mobs.** [Cinder Husks](../mobs/ashen-reach/cinder-husk.md) roam everywhere in small groups.
[Ash Stalkers](../mobs/ashen-reach/ash-stalker.md) lie buried in the ash and burst out when you pass. Rarely, an
[Ashen Carrion](../mobs/ashen-reach/ashen-carrion.md), the zone's elite, circles overhead. More of all three are placed
in the ruins and never come back once killed.

**Ruins.** Off the road you find farmsteads, barns, windmill skeletons, rows of scarecrows (watch for Stalkers), a
pilgrim camp, gallows and empty village altars. Most have a chest of food, iron, gold, {{item embervoid:ash_bomb}}s,
{{item embervoid:amber_balm}} and {{item embervoid:cinder_shard}}s. The zone's **treasure** lies in the vault under a
burnt granary: it can hold diamonds, Smoldering materials, pieces of the [Reaper's Set](../items/armor/reapers-set.md),
the Fields' Reliquary accessories and, rarely, the {{item embervoid:ashen_scythe}}.

**Ore.** [Cinder Ore](../blocks/ores/cinder-ore.md) is the zone's own; it gives Cinder Shards.

**The road.** Two outposts stand on the road, a toll gate and a watchtower, each with Husks and Carrions.

## The boss

At the road's end, a market square sunk into a crater and ringed by burnt granary pillars is the arena of the
[Ash Colossus](../bosses/ash-colossus.md). An [Ember Shrine](../blocks/functional/ember-shrine.md) and a chest wait
outside its fog wall; the shrine wakes once you have beaten the Colossus. The chest can hold
{{item embervoid:ash_of_rematch}}, a {{item embervoid:smoldering_template}}, Ash Bombs, Smoldering Resin and Cinder
Shards.
