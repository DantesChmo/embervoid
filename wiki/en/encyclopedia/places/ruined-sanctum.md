## How to find it

The Ruined Sanctum is the overworld's dungeon. It generates in any land biome, roughly one every 300 to 400 blocks
<!-- structure_set ember_sanctum: spacing 22, separation 8 -->, so a short ride in any direction usually finds one.
From the surface you see only a ruin. It comes in three looks: a courtyard ruin, a collapsed chapel and a sunken
cloister. Each has its own stairwell down.

If you cannot find one, look for a {{item embervoid:sanctum_map}} in the chests of village cartographers and temples,
ruined portals and shipwrecks. It marks the nearest Ruined Sanctum with a red X. `/locate structure embervoid:ember_sanctum`
works too.

## Inside

A stairwell runs down a sealed shaft cased in rock into the gate hall. From there, side halls branch off: corridors,
chambers, crypts and storerooms.

- **Guards.** Most hall posts hold a [Hollow](../mobs/overworld/hollow.md), and some chambers a skeleton archer. They
  are placed when the dungeon generates and do not come back once killed. Bring an axe for the Hollows' guard.
- **Loot.** The hall chests hold ordinary dungeon loot, {{item embervoid:flask_shard}}s and, now and then, one of the
  two Sanctum weapons: the {{item embervoid:sanctum_longsword}} or the {{item embervoid:ashbearers_mace}}.
- **The boss door.** At the end of the halls an [Ember Shrine](../blocks/functional/ember-shrine.md) stands in front of
  a glowing curtain of ember fog. Rest there: it heals you and refills your flask. It does not move your respawn point,
  which stays at your bed.
- **The arena.** Behind the fog is a dim hall ringed by unlit braziers, with a cold
  [Cleft Stele](../blocks/functional/cleft-stele.md) on the dais at its far end. Step in and the
  [Fallen Sister](../bosses/fallen-sister.md) appears.

Each Sanctum's Sister is beaten once per world. Everyone in that fight gets a {{item embervoid:crying_soul}}, and on
your first clear also a Flask Shard and a Sanctum weapon. After that the dungeon stays cleared; she does not return.

The Crying Soul kindles a Cleft Stele: the one in the arena, or one you build yourself. A lit stele takes you to the
[Ember Sanctum](ember-sanctum.md). Only one stele per world burns at a time.

Worlds that began before 0.5.0 may still hold an old Sanctum with its core intact: an Ashen Maiden and a ring of
obelisks instead of a boss arena. Both still work.
