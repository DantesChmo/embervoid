## How to find it

The Royal Woods are the [Ashen Reach](ashen-reach.md) zone north of the [Crossroads](crossroads.md). Take the north road
out of the plaza. Mob levels run from 18 to 21 <!-- Realms.ASHEN_REACH level band zone_b -->.

## What is there

{{mobs-in}}

The king's old hunting grounds: hills, ravines and dry stream beds under a dense grey-green fog, covered in a forest
of dead trees over podzol and moss. Sight lines are short; keep the road in view or carry an
{{item embervoid:ember_lodestar}}.

**Mobs.** [Withered Thralls](../mobs/ashen-reach/withered-thrall.md) wander the woods, and
[Lantern Hags](../mobs/ashen-reach/lantern-hag.md) throw fire flasks from a distance. Some of the dead trees are not
trees at all: [Bark Hollows](../mobs/ashen-reach/bark-hollow.md) stand still in glades and by the park wall until you
come close. More Thralls and Hags are placed in the ruins and never come back once killed.

**Ruins.** Stretches of the broken park wall, kennels, hunters' hides, rows of hanging cages and lanterns, and quiet
glades. Most have a chest of food, iron, gold, {{item embervoid:ash_bomb}}s, {{item embervoid:amber_balm}} and
{{item embervoid:royal_amber}}. The zone's **treasure** is in the loft of the king's hunting lodge: it can hold
diamonds, Smoldering materials, pieces of the [Huntsman's Set](../items/armor/huntsmans-set.md), the Woods' Reliquary
accessories and, rarely, the {{item embervoid:huntsmans_crossbow}}. The Woods have no elite mob, so the lodge chest is
the only source of their accessories.

**Ore.** [Amber Veins](../blocks/ores/amber-vein.md) are the zone's own; they give Royal Amber.

**The road.** Two outposts stand on the road, a toll gate and a watchtower, each with Thralls and Hags.

## The boss

At the road's end, behind the hunting lodge, a walled garden, the Royal Grove, is the arena of the
[Grieving Tree](../bosses/grieving-tree.md). Bring a bow and plenty of arrows. An
[Ember Shrine](../blocks/functional/ember-shrine.md) and a chest wait outside its fog wall; the shrine wakes once you
have beaten the Tree. The chest can hold {{item embervoid:ash_of_rematch}}, a {{item embervoid:smoldering_template}},
Amber Balm, arrows and Royal Amber.
