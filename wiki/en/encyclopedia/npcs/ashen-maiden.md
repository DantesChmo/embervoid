## Where she stands

The Ashen Maiden stands in the [Ember Sanctum](../places/ember-sanctum.md), the hub island, on the south side of its
open core, facing the Ember Shrine and the ring of Void Obelisks. She never leaves her spot, cannot be hurt and never
despawns.

In worlds that began before 0.5.0, the old Sanctum cores that were already generated keep their own Maiden, and she
works the same way there.

Her burnt-out twin, the [Fallen Sister](../bosses/fallen-sister.md), guards every Ruined Sanctum.

## Leveling with her

Right-click her to open the **level-up screen**. It shows:

- your seven stats and what each one gives you now;
- a preview of what the next point in a stat would give;
- what the next level costs, in experience points, and how much experience you have.

Click a stat to buy one point in it. Each click is one level and costs vanilla experience points; the next level costs
a little more. Points are applied at once. Walk away from her and the screen closes.

There is no way to take points back.

How the stats work, what each one does and what levels cost: [Stats and leveling](../../guides/mechanics/stats-and-leveling.md).
