## What it is for

Metal of all three zones, fused in a flame that will not die. With a {{item embervoid:smoldering_template}} it turns
diamond armor into the [Smoldering Plate](../armor/smoldering-set.md) on the smithing table, and two of them go into
the [Royal Ash Rapier](../weapons/royal-ash-rapier.md). On the anvil it repairs every realm weapon and the Smoldering
Plate. It does not burn in lava.
