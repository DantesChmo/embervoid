## What it is for

The healing material of the [Royal Woods](../../places/royal-woods.md). It goes into the
{{item embervoid:amber_balm}}, the {{item embervoid:amber_lantern_oil}}, the {{item embervoid:smoldering_ingot}}, the
{{item embervoid:ash_of_rematch}} and the [Huntsman's Crossbow](../weapons/huntsmans-crossbow.md). It repairs the
[Huntsman's Set](../armor/huntsmans-set.md) on the anvil. Nine make an {{item embervoid:amber_block}}, which turns back
into nine.

## How to get it

{{recipe}}

{{sources}}

Mine {{item embervoid:amber_vein}} in the Woods with an iron pickaxe or better: 1 or 2 amber a block, more with
Fortune. The Woods' mobs drop it now and then, the placed Bark Hollow more often; the
[Grieving Tree](../../bosses/grieving-tree.md) drops a handful, and the Woods' chests hold plenty.
