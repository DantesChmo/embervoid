## What it is for

A smithing template, like netherite's: on the smithing table it turns a diamond armor piece and a
{{item embervoid:smoldering_ingot}} into the matching piece of the [Smoldering Plate](../armor/smoldering-set.md). It
works on armor only, and it is used up, so a full set takes four.

Craft it from a diamond, 6 {{item embervoid:cinder_shard}} and 2 {{item embervoid:ash_stone}}. Every boss arena's
chest holds one, and the treasure chests of the zones and the Citadel often hold another.
