## What it is for

A coating for your blade. Hold a melee weapon (a realm weapon, a sword, an axe or a trident) in one hand and the
resin in the other, and use the resin: for 60 seconds <!-- gear.consumables.resinSeconds --> every melee hit with that
weapon sets the target on fire. The coating stays on the weapon, not on you, so you can switch away and back.
