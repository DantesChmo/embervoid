## What it is for

The fire material of the [Ashen Fields](../../places/ashen-fields.md). It goes into the realm's fire consumables
({{item embervoid:ash_bomb}}, {{item embervoid:smoldering_resin}}), the {{item embervoid:smoldering_template}}, the
{{item embervoid:smoldering_ingot}}, the {{item embervoid:ash_of_rematch}} and the
[Ashen Scythe](../weapons/ashen-scythe.md). It also repairs the [Reaper's Set](../armor/reapers-set.md) on the anvil,
and it is good furnace fuel: one shard burns as long as two coal <!-- gear.consumables.cinderFuelTicks 3200 -->.

## How to get it

{{recipe}}

{{sources}}

Mine {{item embervoid:cinder_ore}} in the Fields with an iron pickaxe or better: 1 to 3 shards a block, more with
Fortune. The Fields' mobs drop a shard now and then, the placed tanks and elites more often; the
[Ash Colossus](../../bosses/ash-colossus.md) and the [Smoldering Lord](../../bosses/smoldering-lord.md) drop a handful,
and the Fields' chests are full of them.
