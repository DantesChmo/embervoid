## What it is for

The armor metal of the [Sunken Necropolis](../../places/sunken-necropolis.md). Smelt or blast
{{item embervoid:raw_grave_iron}} into a {{item embervoid:grave_iron_ingot}}. The ingot is the main part of the
{{item embervoid:smoldering_ingot}} (four per ingot), and goes into the {{item embervoid:whetstone}}, the
[Warden's Scourge](../weapons/wardens-scourge.md) and the grave iron grate and plate. It repairs the
[Warden's Set](../armor/wardens-set.md) on the anvil.

{{sources}}

Mine {{item embervoid:grave_iron_ore}} deep in the Necropolis with a diamond pickaxe or better; Fortune gives more raw
iron. The Necropolis' mobs drop raw grave iron now and then, the placed Hollow Sentinels and Grave Wardens more often,
and the [Crypt Keeper](../../bosses/crypt-keeper.md) drops a few. Ready ingots turn up in the realm's treasure chests.
