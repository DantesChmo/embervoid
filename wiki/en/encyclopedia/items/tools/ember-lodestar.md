## What it is for

A compass for a realm. In [Ashen Reach](../../places/ashen-reach.md) its needle points to the nearest boss arena whose
boss you have not beaten yet; right-click it and it tells you how many blocks away that guardian waits. With every boss
beaten it says so, and outside the realms it is cold and the needle spins.

In 0.5.3 no recipe and no chest gives it: it comes from the creative inventory or a command. Without it, follow the
roads from the [Crossroads](../../places/crossroads.md); each one leads to an arena.
