## What it is for

An explorer map that leads to the nearest [Ruined Sanctum](../../places/ruined-sanctum.md): hold it to see the way, the
dungeon is marked with a red X. It is a vanilla map with its own name, so it works like a treasure map and fills in as
you walk.

It is found, not crafted: the chests of village cartographers and temples, ruined portals and shipwrecks' map rooms
