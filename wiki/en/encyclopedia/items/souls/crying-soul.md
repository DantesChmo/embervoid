## What it is for

What is left of the Fallen Sister, and the key to the [Ember Sanctum](../../places/ember-sanctum.md). Use it on any
stone of a cold [Cleft Stele](../../blocks/functional/cleft-stele.md) to kindle it: the soul is used up, the stele
lights, and its mist leads to the hub. The stele is a ritual circle of {{item embervoid:cleft_stone}}: a centre stone
and four pillars of 3 Cleft Stone, two blocks out from it on each side. Every Ruined Sanctum keeps a cold circle on its
arena's dais.

On anything else, or in a place where steles do not burn (a realm, the hub), nothing happens and you keep the soul.
Only one stele burns per world besides the hub's own: kindling a new one puts the old one out. See
[Kindling the Cleft Stele](../../../guides/walkthrough/cleft-stele.md).
