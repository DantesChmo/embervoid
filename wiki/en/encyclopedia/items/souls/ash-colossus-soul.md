## What it is for

The core of a unique weapon: craft it into the [Ashen Scythe](../weapons/ashen-scythe.md) with 4 {{item embervoid:cinder_shard}} and an iron hoe. Each soul makes one weapon, so think before you craft a second copy
with a soul from a later kill.

{{sources}}

No loot table drops it. The [Ash Colossus](../../bosses/ash-colossus.md) hands it to you in person, straight into your inventory, the first time you beat
it: every player in the fight who had not beaten it before gets their own. The first kill also gives a {{item embervoid:flask_shard}}.

When you help a friend who has not beaten the boss yet, the soul answers you again now and then
<!-- boss.repeatSoulChance 25 % -->. A rematch summoned with {{item embervoid:ash_of_rematch}} never gives the soul.
