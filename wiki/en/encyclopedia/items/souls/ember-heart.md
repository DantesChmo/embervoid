## What it is for

A boss's heart, taken on a first kill; its tooltip names the boss it came from. It is the boss framework's generic
first-kill reward, meant to be traded for that boss's gear. In 0.5.3 every boss has its own reward instead (the
[Crying Soul](crying-soul.md), the realm souls, the {{item embervoid:vampire_fang}}), so the Ember Heart has no use
yet.
