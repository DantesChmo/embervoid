## What it does

A unique chestplate from the [Smoldering Citadel](../../places/smoldering-citadel.md), as strong as a Smoldering
Chestplate. When a hit drops you below 30 % health <!-- gear.uniques.novaHealthFraction -->, the cuirass bursts in a
fire nova: every mob within 4 blocks <!-- gear.uniques.novaRadius --> takes ember damage and is thrown back. Then it
cools down for 60 seconds <!-- gear.uniques.novaCooldownTicks 1200 -->; the cooldown shows on the chestplate like an
item cooldown. Kindling raises the nova's damage.

It is a piece of its own, not part of a set: it never counts toward a set bonus. It does not burn in lava.

## How to get it

{{sources}}

It drops only when you kill a [Furnace Golem](../../mobs/ashen-reach/furnace-golem.md) or a
[Smoldering Knight](../../mobs/ashen-reach/smoldering-knight.md) yourself <!-- gear.loot.eliteUnique 4 % -->. Both are
placed in the Citadel and never come back, so every one you kill is one roll.

## Tips

- The nova buys you the space to drink a flask. Back off and heal the moment it goes off.
