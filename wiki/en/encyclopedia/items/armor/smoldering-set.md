{{stats}}

The crafted armor of [Ashen Reach](../../places/ashen-reach.md): netherite's protection, toughness and knockback
resistance, and every piece also takes a share off the fire damage you take. It does not burn in lava as an item.
It is the armor to aim for before the [Smoldering Citadel](../../places/smoldering-citadel.md), where everything burns.

## Set bonus

{{set-bonus}}

Wear all four pieces and burning stops hurting you: being on fire deals no damage. Standing in lava or in fire still
does, so the set lets you shrug off flames, not swim in magma.

## How to get it

{{recipe}}

{{sources}}

Each piece is an upgrade of the matching **diamond** piece on the smithing table, like netherite: the diamond piece, a
{{item embervoid:smoldering_template}} and a {{item embervoid:smoldering_ingot}}. The template is used up, so a full
set takes four templates and four ingots. The ingot needs materials from all three zones, so the plate comes together
late in the realm. Repair it with Smoldering Ingots on the anvil.

## Tips

- Keep your enchanted diamond armor: the smithing table carries its enchantments over to the plate.
- Craft the chestplate first if you run short; the pieces' fire reduction adds up one by one.
