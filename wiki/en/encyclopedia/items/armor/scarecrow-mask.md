## What it does

A unique helmet from the [Ashen Fields](../../places/ashen-fields.md). While you wear it, mobs notice you at half the
usual distance <!-- gear.uniques.scarecrowDetection 0.5 -->, the way a mob head hides you in vanilla, and
[Ash Stalkers](../../mobs/ashen-reach/ash-stalker.md) never ambush you.

It is a piece of its own, not part of a set: it never counts toward a set bonus, so wearing it costs you the bonus of
whatever set you had on. It does not burn in lava.

## How to get it

{{sources}}

It drops only when you kill a Fields mob yourself: very rarely from a [Cinder Husk](../../mobs/ashen-reach/cinder-husk.md)
or an Ash Stalker <!-- gear.loot.fodderUnique 0.5 % -->, more often from the placed
[Ashen Carrion](../../mobs/ashen-reach/ashen-carrion.md) <!-- gear.loot.eliteUnique 4 % -->. Looting helps a little.
