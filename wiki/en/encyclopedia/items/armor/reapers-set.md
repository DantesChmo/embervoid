{{stats}}

The [Ashen Fields](../../places/ashen-fields.md) set: Reaper's Hood, Reaper's Smock, Reaper's Trousers and Reaper's
Clogs. It is light armor with no toughness, worn for speed: the fastest of the three zone sets for moving between
fights, dodging boss slams and outrunning a pack.

## Set bonus

{{set-bonus}}

With all four pieces on you move faster. The bonus is on your movement speed itself, so it adds to sprinting and to
Speed effects.

## How to get it

{{recipe}}

{{sources}}

The pieces are not crafted. They drop from the Fields' mobs when you kill them: now and then from a
[Cinder Husk](../../mobs/ashen-reach/cinder-husk.md) <!-- gear.loot.fodderSet 3 % -->, more often from the placed
[Ashen Carrion](../../mobs/ashen-reach/ashen-carrion.md) <!-- gear.loot.eliteSet 15 % -->. Looting raises both. The
Fields' treasure chest holds a piece fairly often <!-- fields_treasure: 30 % -->, and the Citadel's treasury can hold a
piece of any zone set. Repair it with {{item embervoid:cinder_shard}} on the anvil.

## Tips

- Swap to the set for travel and back to heavier armor for a boss, or keep it on for fights you win by dodging.
- The [Courier's Ring](../accessories/couriers-ring.md) stacks with it for even faster sprints.
