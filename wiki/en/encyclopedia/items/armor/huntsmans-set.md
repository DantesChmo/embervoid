{{stats}}

The [Royal Woods](../../places/royal-woods.md) set: Huntsman's Cap, Huntsman's Jerkin, Huntsman's Breeches and
Huntsman's Boots. Medium leather with a little toughness, made for fighting at range.

## Set bonus

{{set-bonus}}

With all four pieces on, every projectile you shoot hits harder: arrows, bolts and tridents alike. It is the set for
the [Huntsman's Crossbow](../weapons/huntsmans-crossbow.md) and for any bow build.

## How to get it

{{recipe}}

{{sources}}

The pieces are not crafted. They drop from the Woods' mobs when you kill them: now and then from a
[Withered Thrall](../../mobs/ashen-reach/withered-thrall.md) or a [Lantern Hag](../../mobs/ashen-reach/lantern-hag.md)
<!-- gear.loot.fodderSet 3 % -->, more often from the placed [Bark Hollow](../../mobs/ashen-reach/bark-hollow.md)
<!-- gear.loot.eliteSet 15 % -->. Looting raises both. The hunting lodge's treasure chest holds a piece fairly often
<!-- woods_treasure: 30 % -->, and the Citadel's treasury can hold a piece of any zone set. Repair it with
{{item embervoid:royal_amber}} on the anvil.

## Tips

- The bonus only counts with the full set; a single missing piece turns it off.
- Mix in the [Huntsman's Greaves](huntsman-greaves.md) only if you give up the set bonus for its speed burst.
