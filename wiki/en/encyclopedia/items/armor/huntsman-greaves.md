## What it does

Unique leggings from the [Royal Woods](../../places/royal-woods.md). Every kill gives you Speed I for 4 seconds
<!-- gear.uniques.greavesSpeedTicks 80 -->, so you can chain from one mob to the next. Falls also hurt less: you take
no damage from a fall of up to 7 blocks <!-- gear.uniques.greavesSafeFallDamage 4 -->, and longer falls hurt that
much less.

It is a piece of its own, not part of a set: it never counts toward a set bonus. It does not burn in lava.

## How to get it

{{sources}}

It drops only when you kill a Woods mob yourself: very rarely from a
[Withered Thrall](../../mobs/ashen-reach/withered-thrall.md) or a [Lantern Hag](../../mobs/ashen-reach/lantern-hag.md)
<!-- gear.loot.fodderUnique 0.5 % -->, more often from the placed [Bark Hollow](../../mobs/ashen-reach/bark-hollow.md)
<!-- gear.loot.eliteUnique 4 % -->. Looting helps a little.
