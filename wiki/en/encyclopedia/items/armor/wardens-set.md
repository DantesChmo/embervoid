{{stats}}

The [Sunken Necropolis](../../places/sunken-necropolis.md) set: Warden's Helm, Warden's Hauberk, Warden's Tassets and
Warden's Boots. The heaviest of the zone sets, with the most armor and toughness; every piece slows you a little
<!-- GearArmor.NECROPOLIS_WARDEN warden_weight: −1.25 % per piece -->. It is the set for standing your ground behind a
shield.

## Set bonus

{{set-bonus}}

With all four pieces on you are harder to knock back, and you take less damage while you block with a shield.

## How to get it

{{recipe}}

{{sources}}

The pieces are not crafted. They drop from the Necropolis' mobs when you kill them: now and then from
[Restless Bones](../../mobs/ashen-reach/restless-bones.md) <!-- gear.loot.fodderSet 3 % -->, more often from the
placed [Hollow Sentinel](../../mobs/ashen-reach/hollow-sentinel.md) and
[Grave Warden](../../mobs/ashen-reach/grave-warden.md) <!-- gear.loot.eliteSet 15 % -->. Looting raises both. The
lower crypt's treasure chest holds a piece fairly often <!-- necropolis_treasure: 30 % -->, and the Citadel's treasury
can hold a piece of any zone set. Repair it with {{item embervoid:grave_iron_ingot}} on the anvil.

## Tips

- The blocking bonus works with any shield; hold it up through a boss's combo and counter in the gap.
- The slowdown is small but real; take the set off for long walks.
