## What it does

Unique boots from the [Sunken Necropolis](../../places/sunken-necropolis.md), the only zone with water. While you wear
them you walk through water at full speed, as with Depth Strider III; Depth Strider on the boots adds nothing more.

They are a piece of their own, not part of a set: they never count toward a set bonus. They do not burn in lava.

## How to get it

{{sources}}

They drop only when you kill a Necropolis mob yourself: very rarely from
[Restless Bones](../../mobs/ashen-reach/restless-bones.md), a [Crypt Moth](../../mobs/ashen-reach/crypt-moth.md) or a
[Hollow Sentinel](../../mobs/ashen-reach/hollow-sentinel.md) <!-- gear.loot.fodderUnique 0.5 % -->, more often from
the placed [Grave Warden](../../mobs/ashen-reach/grave-warden.md) <!-- gear.loot.eliteUnique 4 % -->. Looting helps a
little.

## Tips

- The {{item embervoid:drowned_mans_ring}} lets you breathe under water; together they make the flooded sinkhole easy.
