## What makes it itself

The duellist's blade. It thrusts, never sweeps, swings fast and reaches a little further than a sword. Its strength is
the **ramp**: fully charged hits in a row on the same target deal +10 % each, up to +30 %
<!-- gear.weapons.rampPerHit, rampMax -->, as long as each lands within 1.5 seconds of the last
<!-- gear.weapons.rampWindowTicks 30 -->. A swing that misses, a hit you take, or a switch to another target starts it
over; a hit at less than full charge neither builds nor breaks it. Amber motes thicken along the blade as it climbs,
and the peak rings out.

Every hit adds a level of Bleeding and sets the target on fire. Part of its damage is ember: that part scales with
Kindling, the rest with Dexterity. It takes the sword enchantments except Sweeping Edge, does not burn in lava, and is
repaired with a {{item embervoid:smoldering_ingot}} on the anvil or a {{item embervoid:whetstone}}.

## Special attack

**Lunge.** Right-click to dash 4 blocks forward <!-- gear.weapons.lungeDash --> and end in a thrust that reaches past
the end of the dash. The thrust hits for the rapier's full damage, adds two levels of Bleeding instead of one and sets
the target on fire. Use it to close a gap, or to finish a fleeing mob.

## Tips

- Pick one target and stay on it. Every full-charge hit counts; mashing at half charge wastes the ramp.
- Dodge rather than trade: a hit taken resets the ramp.
- Put points into Dexterity first and Kindling second; the {{item embervoid:amber_tear}} helps the ember part.
