## What makes it itself

The crowd cutter. The scythe reaches a little further than a sword, and **every hit sweeps**: the mobs standing
around your target take three quarters of the hit as well <!-- gear.weapons.sweepShare 0.75, sweepRadius 2.5 -->. A
**fully charged** swing also sends an ash wave rolling forward, 6 blocks long and 3 wide
<!-- gear.weapons.waveLength, waveWidth -->, that burns everything in its path with ember damage (Kindling raises it).

Each enemy a sweep catches gives you 3 stamina back <!-- gear.weapons.sweepStamina -->. On a single target the scythe
is the weakest of the realm weapons; on a group it is by far the best.

It scales with Strength and Dexterity, takes the sword enchantments except Sweeping Edge, does not burn in lava, and is
repaired with a {{item embervoid:smoldering_ingot}} on the anvil or a {{item embervoid:whetstone}}.

## Special attack

**Harvest.** Right-click to spin a full circle. Every mob within 3.5 blocks <!-- gear.weapons.harvestRadius --> takes
the scythe's full damage and is pushed back a little. Like the sweep, each enemy caught gives stamina back, which
softens the spin's cost when you use it in a crowd.

## Tips

- Fight in the middle of the pack, not at its edge: more mobs around your target means more sweep hits and more stamina.
- Wait for the full charge when mobs line up in front of you; the ash wave hits the ones behind.
- Pair it with the [Reaper's Set](../armor/reapers-set.md) and the {{item embervoid:ring_of_embers}} for the Fields and
  the Citadel's crowds.
