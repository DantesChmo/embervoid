## What makes it itself

A guard's blade from the halls of the [Ruined Sanctum](../../places/ruined-sanctum.md), and the first weapon in the mod
with a special attack. It sits between an iron and a diamond sword, so it is worth carrying from the first dungeon
until the realm hands you something better.

It scales with Dexterity. It is not a vanilla sword: it does not sweep on its own, but it takes the sword
enchantments (Sharpness, Smite, Bane of Arthropods, Fire Aspect, Looting, Knockback) at the table and the anvil. You
repair it with iron ingots.

## Special attack

**Wide Cleave.** Right-click to swing once through a wide arc in front of you. Everything in a 90° arc out to 3 blocks
<!-- sanctumGear.cleaveArc, sanctumGear.cleaveRange --> takes a quarter more than your normal hit
<!-- sanctumGear.cleaveMultiplier 1.25 -->, and the hit goes through armor, mob levels and your Dexterity scaling like
any other. It only hits what you can see: no cleaving through walls.

The cleave costs stamina and has almost no cooldown, so how often you use it is up to your stamina bar. See
[Stamina and special attacks](../../../guides/mechanics/stamina-and-specials.md).

There is no recipe. Each outer-hall chest of a Ruined Sanctum has a small chance to hold either the Longsword or the
{{item embervoid:ashbearers_mace}} (never both) <!-- chests/sanctum_outer_hall: 6 % each -->, and the first time you
beat the [Fallen Sister](../../bosses/fallen-sister.md) you get one of the two at random.

## Tips

- Turn into a group of Hollows and cleave: one right-click reaches every mob in front of you.
- Raise Endurance if you lean on Wide Cleave; it is limited by stamina, not by its cooldown.
- If you pick Strength instead, the [Ashbearer's Mace](ashbearers-mace.md) is the other Sanctum weapon.
