## What makes it itself

The hunter's bolt. It loads slower than a vanilla crossbow, but each bolt hits far harder, throws the target back as
hard as Punch III and passes through two more mobs <!-- gear.crossbow.punch, gear.crossbow.pierce -->. A bolt into a
mob that is **not after you** deals ×1.5 <!-- gear.crossbow.openerMultiplier -->: the opener, for a mob that has not
seen you yet or is busy with someone else.

It scales with Dexterity and Strength in equal parts. Quick Charge still shortens the load. It does not burn in lava
and is repaired with a {{item embervoid:smoldering_ingot}} on the anvil or a {{item embervoid:whetstone}}.

## Special attack

None. Right-click loads and fires, as on any crossbow.

## Tips

- Open every fight with a bolt from cover: the first shot on an unaware mob is the opener.
- Line mobs up in a corridor; one bolt pierces three of them.
- The full [Huntsman's Set](../armor/huntsmans-set.md) adds to projectile damage, bolts included.
- In a party, shoot whatever is chasing your friend: it is not after you, so every bolt is an opener.
