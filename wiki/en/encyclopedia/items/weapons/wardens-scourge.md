## What makes it itself

A real whip. Its lash reaches twice as far as a sword and **hits every enemy along it**, not just the one you point
at; the lash stops at the first block in its way. Every swing is a crack, even one at the air, and it costs one
durability however many mobs it bites.

The **tip** is the point. A mob caught by the last 1.5 blocks of the lash <!-- gear.weapons.whipTipBand --> takes
×1.6 damage <!-- gear.weapons.whipTipMultiplier -->, is stopped where it stands for half a second and loses the attack
it was winding up <!-- gear.weapons.whipTipStunTicks 10 -->. You hear and see the difference: a tip hit cracks with a
flash and a shockwave.

Every hit adds a level of Bleeding (up to III), which hurts every second through armor. Some mobs never bleed:
Restless Bones, Furnace Golems, Bark Hollows, the Ash Colossus and the Grieving Tree.

It scales with Dexterity, takes the sword enchantments except Sweeping Edge, does not burn in lava, and is repaired
with a {{item embervoid:smoldering_ingot}} on the anvil or a {{item embervoid:whetstone}}.

## Special attack

**Lash.** Right-click to snap the whip at the first mob in its reach and drag it toward you, up to 4 blocks
<!-- gear.weapons.lashPull -->; it stops short of your feet. Knockback resistance lessens the pull, so heavy mobs barely
move.

## Tips

- Keep your distance: step back until your target sits at the end of the lash and every swing lands with the tip.
- A tip hit cancels a wind-up. Time it on a mob's tell and it never gets the blow off.
- Lash an archer or a caster out of its group, then crack it at range as it stumbles in.
