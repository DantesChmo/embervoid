## What makes it itself

A heavy, slow mace from the [Ruined Sanctum](../../places/ruined-sanctum.md), the Strength counterpart of the
[Sanctum Longsword](sanctum-longsword.md). It hits harder and swings slower; like the Longsword it sits between iron
and diamond gear.

It scales with Strength, and it breaks a Hollow's raised guard the way an axe does, so a blocking
[Hollow](../../mobs/overworld/hollow.md) cannot turtle against it. It takes the sword enchantments (Sharpness, Smite,
Bane of Arthropods, Fire Aspect, Looting, Knockback) and is repaired with iron ingots.

## Special attack

**Ground Slam.** Right-click to slam the mace into the ground. Every mob within 3 blocks around you
<!-- sanctumGear.slamRadius --> takes three quarters of your normal hit <!-- sanctumGear.slamMultiplier 0.75 -->, is
thrown back and gets Slowness II for 2 seconds <!-- sanctumGear.slamSlownessTicks 40, slamSlownessAmplifier 1 -->.
It only hits what you can see.

The slam is the most expensive special in stamina, with almost no cooldown. See
[Stamina and special attacks](../../../guides/mechanics/stamina-and-specials.md).

There is no recipe. Each outer-hall chest of a Ruined Sanctum has a small chance to hold either the Mace or the
{{item embervoid:sanctum_longsword}} (never both) <!-- chests/sanctum_outer_hall: 6 % each -->, and the first time
you beat the [Fallen Sister](../../bosses/fallen-sister.md) you get one of the two at random.

## Tips

- Slam when you are surrounded: it clears space and the Slowness gives you time to drink or reposition.
- Use plain hits on guarding Hollows; the mace opens their guard where a sword would bounce off.
