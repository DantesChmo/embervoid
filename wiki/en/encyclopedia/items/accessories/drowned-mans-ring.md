## What it does

You breathe under water, like water breathing.

Wear it in a ring slot of the [Reliquary](../../../guides/mechanics/reliquary.md). You have two ring slots and cannot wear two of the same ring; a ring goes into your Ash Remnant with the rest of your gear when you die. Together with the [Drowned Sabatons](../armor/drowned-sabatons.md) the flooded sinkhole is no obstacle.
