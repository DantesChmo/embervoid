## What it does

While worn it adds +1 to every one of the seven stats <!-- reliquary.artifacts.sealPoints -->.

Wear it in an artifact slot of the [Reliquary](../../../guides/mechanics/reliquary.md). The points count in every effect of the stat, but not in your character level, the cost of your next level or the stat cap. You have two artifact slots and cannot wear two of the same artifact; points from two different artifacts on the same stat add up. An artifact goes into your Ash Remnant with the rest of your gear when you die. The Seal's point adds to a one-stat artifact worn beside it.
