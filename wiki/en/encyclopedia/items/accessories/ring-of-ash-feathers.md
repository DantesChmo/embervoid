## What it does

You fall slowly, and a fall of up to 8 blocks does no damage <!-- reliquary.rings.featherSafeFall -->; longer falls hurt that much less. Hold sneak to drop at full speed. The slow fall pauses while you fly, glide, swim or climb.

Wear it in a ring slot of the [Reliquary](../../../guides/mechanics/reliquary.md). You have two ring slots and cannot wear two of the same ring; a ring goes into your Ash Remnant with the rest of your gear when you die. The fall protection adds to the [Huntsman's Greaves](../armor/huntsman-greaves.md)' and to Feather Falling.
