## What it does

Your melee hits set the target on fire for 2 seconds <!-- reliquary.rings.embersFireSeconds -->. Mobs immune to fire do not burn.

Wear it in a ring slot of the [Reliquary](../../../guides/mechanics/reliquary.md). You have two ring slots and cannot wear two of the same ring; a ring goes into your Ash Remnant with the rest of your gear when you die. It works with any melee weapon. A weapon that already burns (Fire Aspect, the [Royal Ash Rapier](../weapons/royal-ash-rapier.md), {{item embervoid:smoldering_resin}}) gains little from it.
