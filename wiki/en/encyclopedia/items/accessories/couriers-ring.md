## What it does

While you sprint you run 15 % faster <!-- reliquary.rings.courierSprintBonus -->. Walking speed does not change.

Wear it in a ring slot of the [Reliquary](../../../guides/mechanics/reliquary.md). You have two ring slots and cannot wear two of the same ring; a ring goes into your Ash Remnant with the rest of your gear when you die. The bonus multiplies your whole speed, so it adds to the [Reaper's Set](../armor/reapers-set.md) and Speed effects.
