## What it does

You mine the realm's ores faster, as with Haste I <!-- reliquary.rings.delverHasteLevel 1 -->: {{item embervoid:cinder_ore}}, {{item embervoid:amber_vein}} and {{item embervoid:grave_iron_ore}}. Other blocks mine at the usual speed.

Wear it in a ring slot of the [Reliquary](../../../guides/mechanics/reliquary.md). You have two ring slots and cannot wear two of the same ring; a ring goes into your Ash Remnant with the rest of your gear when you die.
