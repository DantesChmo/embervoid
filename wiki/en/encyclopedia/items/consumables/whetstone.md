## What it is for

A field repair for the realm's unique weapons. Hold the weapon in one hand and the whetstone in the other and use the
whetstone: it restores a quarter of the weapon's durability <!-- gear.consumables.whetstoneShare -->. It works on the
[Ashen Scythe](../weapons/ashen-scythe.md), [Warden's Scourge](../weapons/wardens-scourge.md),
[Royal Ash Rapier](../weapons/royal-ash-rapier.md) and [Huntsman's Crossbow](../weapons/huntsmans-crossbow.md), not
on the Sanctum weapons or vanilla gear, and not on a weapon that is already undamaged.
