## What it is for

Calls a beaten zone boss back. Use it on the [Ember Shrine](../../blocks/functional/ember-shrine.md) outside a boss
arena of [Ashen Reach](../../places/ashen-reach.md) whose boss you have beaten: the ash stirs, and whoever steps into
the arena within a minute summons the boss again. The rematch gives the boss's loot and XP, never its soul or a Flask
Shard; a player in it who had never beaten the boss still gets a real first kill. It does not work on a boss you have
not beaten, or while the arena is already busy. See [Boss fights](../../../guides/mechanics/boss-fights.md).
