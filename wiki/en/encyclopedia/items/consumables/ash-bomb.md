## What it is for

A throwing bomb. Throw it like a snowball: where it lands it bursts into a ring of fire that deals ember damage to
every mob within 2 blocks <!-- gear.consumables.bombRadius --> and sets them on fire. It sets no blocks alight and
spares your teammates. Kindling raises its damage.
