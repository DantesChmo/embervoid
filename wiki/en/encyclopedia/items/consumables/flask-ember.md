## What it is for

A spark of the king's hoarded flame. Use it and your [Healing Flask](healing-flask.md) drinks it: from then on every
sip heals 15 % more <!-- gear.consumables.flaskEmberBonus -->, for good, through death. It works once per player; a
second ember is refused and you keep it.

in the fight who had not beaten it before gets one.
