## What it is for

Your healing. Hold use to drink: a sip spends one charge and heals you over a moment
<!-- flask.healAmount 8 HP, flask.drinkTicks 32 -->; stop early and you lose the rest of that sip. You cannot drink
while sprinting. You start with 3 charges <!-- flask.baseCharges -->; the tooltip shows how many are left and how much
a sip heals.

The charges belong to you, not to the flask: a second flask adds nothing. They fill again when you rest at an
[Ember Shrine](../../blocks/functional/ember-shrine.md), when you sleep through a night and when you respawn. The flask
never drops on death and never goes into your Ash Remnant. Kindling makes each sip heal more, a
{{item embervoid:flask_shard}} adds a charge, and the {{item embervoid:flask_ember}} strengthens every sip for good.
More in [Healing Flask](../../../guides/mechanics/healing-flask.md).
