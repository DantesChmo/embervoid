## What it is for

Fuel for an [Ember Lantern](../../blocks/functional/ember-lantern.md) you carry. Hold the lantern in your offhand and
the oil in your main hand, then use the oil: the lantern burns for 10 minutes <!-- gear.consumables.oilMinutes -->.
While a lit lantern is in your offhand you see in the dark as with Night Vision, which is what the crypts of the
[Sunken Necropolis](../../places/sunken-necropolis.md) and the [Crypt Keeper](../../bosses/crypt-keeper.md)'s darkness
call for. The oil is on the lantern, so you can put it away and take it out again while time is left.
