## What it is for

Offer it at an [Ember Shrine](../../blocks/functional/ember-shrine.md) (use it on the shrine) to give your
[Healing Flask](healing-flask.md) one more charge, up to 8 <!-- flask.maxCharges -->. The new charge comes filled; the
others fill on your next rest. At the cap the shrine refuses the shard and you keep it.

## How to get it

{{recipe}}

{{sources}}

- Outer-hall chests of a [Ruined Sanctum](../../places/ruined-sanctum.md) hold one now and then.
- Your first clear of a [Fallen Sister](../../bosses/fallen-sister.md) gives one.
- The first kill of each zone boss of [Ashen Reach](../../places/ashen-reach.md) (the Ash Colossus, the Grieving Tree
  and the Crypt Keeper) gives one.
- The [Vampire](../../bosses/vampire.md)'s first kill gives two <!-- vampire.firstKillShards -->, a later kill
  sometimes one.
- Beating your ghost wave on a [full moon night](../../../guides/mechanics/moon-nights.md) leaves one behind.

Boss rewards go straight into your inventory.
