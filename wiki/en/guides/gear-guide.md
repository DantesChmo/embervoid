Embervoid's gear climbs in three steps: vanilla gear in the overworld, the Sanctum weapons from the dungeon, and the
realm gear of Ashen Reach, which ends well above netherite.

## The ladder

| Step | Weapons | Armor |
|---|---|---|
| Overworld | vanilla, up to netherite | vanilla, up to netherite |
| Ruined Sanctum | Sanctum Longsword, Ashbearer's Mace: between iron and diamond, but with a special attack | vanilla |
| Ashen Reach zones | Ashen Scythe, Huntsman's Crossbow, Warden's Scourge: about 30 % above netherite | Reaper's, Huntsman's and Warden's sets (iron to diamond), unique pieces |
| Smoldering Citadel | Royal Ash Rapier | Smoldering Plate (netherite numbers, fire protection) |

The Sanctum weapons are not stronger than a diamond sword on paper. What they give you early is a special attack, and
so a reason to put points into Endurance before you ever reach a realm.

The realm melee weapons take the sword enchantments (Sharpness, Smite, Bane of Arthropods, Fire Aspect, Looting, Knockback)
on the anvil and the enchanting table, so an enchanted realm weapon stays ahead of an enchanted netherite sword.

Rough builds:

- **Strength:** Ashbearer's Mace, then the Ashen Scythe. Wear the Warden's Chain.
- **Dexterity:** Sanctum Longsword, then the Warden's Scourge, and the Royal Ash Rapier at the end. Wear the Royal
  Hound Fang.
- **Ranged:** a vanilla bow or crossbow on Dexterity, then the Huntsman's Crossbow with the Huntsman's set (+15 %
  projectile damage).
- **Everyone:** Vitality for health, Endurance if you lean on specials, Kindling for a stronger flask.

The weapons' numbers, passives and recipes are on their encyclopedia pages.

## Where each piece comes from

- **Sanctum weapons:** Ruined Sanctum outer-hall chests (rare), and one guaranteed on your first clear of a Sanctum.
- **Realm weapons:** craft them from a boss soul and the zone's material (see [Ashen Reach](walkthrough/ashen-reach.md)),
  or find one in that zone's treasure chest.
- **Zone armor sets:** drops from that zone's monsters, and the treasure chests. See
  [Armor sets](mechanics/armor-sets.md).
- **Unique armor pieces:** rare drops from their zone's monsters, likelier from its elite.
- **Smoldering Plate:** smithing. The {{item embervoid:smoldering_ingot}} takes 4 Grave Iron Ingots, 2 Cinder Shards
  and 2 Royal Amber, so you need all three zones' ores. The {{item embervoid:smoldering_template}} is crafted from
  Cinder Shards, ash stone and a diamond, and turns up in arena and treasure chests.
- **Rings and artifacts:** treasure chests and elites. See [The Reliquary](mechanics/reliquary.md).

## Consumables

Ashen Reach chests are full of them:

- {{item embervoid:ash_bomb}}: thrown, it bursts into a ring of fire.
- {{item embervoid:smoldering_resin}}: coats the weapon in your other hand; for 60 s its hits set foes on fire.
- {{item embervoid:amber_balm}}: cures Bleeding and Poison, then heals slowly.
- {{item embervoid:amber_lantern_oil}}: pour it into an Ember Lantern held in your offhand; it burns for 10 minutes and
  lights the dark.
- {{item embervoid:whetstone}}: restores a quarter of a realm weapon's durability (hold the weapon in your other hand).
