Embervoid is a dark fantasy adventure for Minecraft 1.20.1 on Fabric, in the spirit of Demon's Souls. The world is
fading: the overworld turns hostile, ruined dungeons hide a burnt-out guardian, and beyond a kindled stele lies a hub
from which obelisks lead into realms of ash, each with its own zones, monsters and bosses.

There are no classes. You grow by spending vanilla experience on seven stats, one point at a time. You heal with a
flask that has charges, not with stacks of food. Your stamina belongs to your weapon's special attacks. When you die,
your XP and items wait for you in an Ash Remnant where you fell.

## The loop

1. **The overworld gets harder.** Every hostile mob has a level, and three new hostiles hunt you. See
   [A harder overworld](walkthrough/harder-overworld.md).
2. **Find a Ruined Sanctum.** Fight down through its halls to the [Fallen Sister](../encyclopedia/bosses/fallen-sister.md).
   She drops the {{item embervoid:crying_soul}}.
3. **Kindle a Cleft Stele** with the soul. It opens the way to the hub.
4. **The Ember Sanctum**, the hub: level up with the [Ashen Maiden](../encyclopedia/npcs/ashen-maiden.md), rest at the
   Ember Shrine, and step through the lit Void Obelisk.
5. **Ashen Reach**, the first realm: three zones in any order, a boss in each, then the Citadel Gate opens and the
   Smoldering Lord waits in his burning city.
6. **Back to the hub**, stronger, with gear well above netherite.

Moon nights interrupt all of this every few weeks of game time: a full moon that sends your curse after you, and a
blood new moon with a Vampire.
