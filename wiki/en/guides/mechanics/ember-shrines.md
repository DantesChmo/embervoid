An [Ember Shrine](../../encyclopedia/blocks/functional/ember-shrine.md) is Embervoid's bonfire. You cannot craft or break one; they stand where the world puts them.

## Where they are

- in the hub, the Ember Sanctum;
- at the boss door of every Ruined Sanctum;
- on the landing of Ashen Reach, outside each boss arena, and in the Smoldering Citadel (the first beside the end of
  the gate road).

## Resting

Right-click a shrine to rest. You get:

- full health,
- a full Healing Flask.

Resting does not bring killed monsters back.

You can also **offer a Flask Shard** at any shrine you can rest at: use the shard on it.

## Arena shrines

The shrine outside a boss arena is **cold** for you until you beat that boss: smoke and ash, no flame, and "The flame
does not answer you" when you try to rest. The moment you win, it kindles for you. Each player sees their own state.

An arena shrine is also where you use an Ash of Rematch (see [Boss fights](boss-fights.md)).

## Respawn in a realm

In a realm, resting at a shrine also makes it your **respawn point** there:

- Die in a realm and you wake at the last shrine you rested at in that realm.
- If you have not rested anywhere yet, you wake at the realm's landing.
- The Smoldering Citadel counts on its own: die inside it and you wake at the last shrine you rested at inside the
  Citadel, or at its first shrine by the gate road.
- Beds in a realm never set your spawn, and your overworld bed spawn is untouched by all of this.

Shrines in the hub and in Ruined Sanctums heal and refill but **do not** set a respawn point: dying near them sends you
to your bed as usual.

## Travel

A lit Void Obelisk in the hub takes you to the last shrine you rested at in its realm. Sneak while you use it to go to
the landing instead.
