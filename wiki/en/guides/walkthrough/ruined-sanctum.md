The [Ruined Sanctum](../../encyclopedia/places/ruined-sanctum.md) is the overworld dungeon that leads into Embervoid's
story. At its bottom waits the first boss, and she carries the key to the hub.

## Finding one

Sanctums are common: about one every 300–400 blocks <!-- structure_set ember_sanctum: spacing 22, separation 8 -->,
on land in plains, forests, taigas, jungles, deserts, savannas, swamps, badlands, meadows, hills and cherry groves.
Look for a ruin on the surface: crumbling ash-grey stone walls in one of three shapes, a courtyard ruin, a collapsed
chapel or a sunken cloister.

The faster way is a {{item embervoid:sanctum_map}}. It is an explorer map with a red X on the nearest Sanctum.
Look for one in:

- village cartographer chests (the most likely place),
- shipwreck map chests,
- village temple chests,
- ruined portal chests.

## Going down

The ruin hides a stairwell in a sealed shaft. At the bottom:

1. **The gate hall**, guarded.
2. **Side halls**: corridors, a chamber, a crypt and a storeroom. Each guard spot may hold a
   [Hollow](../../encyclopedia/mobs/overworld/hollow.md), and chambers may hold a skeleton archer. Bring an axe or an
   {{item embervoid:ashbearers_mace}} for the Hollows' guard.
3. **An Ember Shrine** at the boss door. Right-click it to rest: full health and a full flask. It does not set your
   respawn point; if you die, you wake at your bed.
4. **A fog wall**, and the boss arena behind it.

## What the halls hold

Search every chest before you open the fog wall. Outer-hall chests hold vanilla supplies and, with luck:

- {{item embervoid:flask_shard}}: offer it at the shrine to add a charge to your flask. Take every one you find.
- One of the two Sanctum weapons, a rare find:
  - {{item embervoid:sanctum_longsword}} (Dexterity), whose right-click Wide Cleave hits everything in an arc in front;
  - {{item embervoid:ashbearers_mace}} (Strength), whose right-click Ground Slam knocks back and slows everything
    around you, and which breaks a Hollow's guard.

Both sit between iron and diamond and have special attacks that spend stamina. See
[Stamina and special attacks](../mechanics/stamina-and-specials.md).
