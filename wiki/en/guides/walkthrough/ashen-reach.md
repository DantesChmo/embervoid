[Ashen Reach](../../encyclopedia/places/ashen-reach.md) is the first realm: a burnt kingdom under an eternal dusk.
Three zones surround a central crossroads, each with its own monsters, ruins, ore and boss. Beat all three bosses and
the Citadel Gate opens on the fourth part of the realm.

## Arriving

Step through the lit Void Obelisk in the hub. You land on the [Crossroads](../../encyclopedia/places/crossroads.md)
plaza:

- the **Ember Shrine** in the middle: rest here before you set out;
- the [Return Obelisk](../../encyclopedia/blocks/functional/return-obelisk.md) to the north: it takes you back to the
  hub;
- the [Citadel Gate](../../encyclopedia/blocks/functional/citadel-gate.md) to the south: a fog wall that stays closed
  for now.

The landing and the boss arenas are protected. Everywhere else you can mine and build.

Ash-brick roads lead from the plaza to each zone's boss arena, with a milestone every 100 blocks and toll gates and
watchtowers along the way. The arenas lie a few hundred blocks out. The realm ends in cliffs and broken bridges over
the void, at [Kingdom's Edge](../../encyclopedia/places/kingdoms-edge.md); go too far and the void pushes you back.

<!-- levels: Realms.ASHEN_REACH level bands -->

You can take them in any order. The suggested order is the one in the table, following the levels: the Fields first,
the Necropolis last. Each zone's boss sits at the top of its zone's band, so a boss you can handle usually means you
are ready for the next zone.

Farmsteads, a windmill, a hunting lodge, mausoleums, chapels and a crypt under the Necropolis hold chests and
**placed enemies**: guards that stand at their posts and never come back once killed. The treasure chest of each zone
can hold its armor set and even its unique weapon.

## Shrines and dying here

In the realm, resting at an Ember Shrine also sets your **respawn point**. If you die, you wake at the last shrine you
rested at in the realm, or at the landing. Your overworld bed is untouched. Rest at every shrine you pass.

Each arena has a shrine outside its fog wall. It stays cold and "does not answer you" until you have beaten that
arena's boss; then it is yours to rest at.

## What you can make along the way

Each zone boss gives you its soul the first time you beat it, plus a {{item embervoid:flask_shard}}. A soul and the
zone's material make that zone's weapon:

- {{item embervoid:ashen_scythe}}: Soul of the Ash Colossus, 4 Cinder Shards and an iron hoe.
- {{item embervoid:huntsmans_crossbow}}: Soul of the Grieving Tree, 4 Royal Amber and a crossbow.
- {{item embervoid:wardens_scourge}}: Soul of the Crypt Keeper, 4 Grave Iron Ingots and a chain.

The three ores together make the {{item embervoid:smoldering_ingot}}, and with a
{{item embervoid:smoldering_template}} it turns diamond armor into the Smoldering Plate. The zones' monsters drop
pieces of their zone's armor set. The whole ladder is in the [Gear guide](../gear-guide.md).
