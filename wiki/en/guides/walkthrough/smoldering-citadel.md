The fourth part of Ashen Reach is the [Smoldering Citadel](../../encyclopedia/places/smoldering-citadel.md): a burning
city on a hill, and in its courtyard the realm's lord.

## Opening the gate

The [Citadel Gate](../../encyclopedia/blocks/functional/citadel-gate.md) is the fog wall on the south rim of the
landing plaza. It opens for each player on their own, once **they** have beaten all three zone bosses. Right-click it
to check: it says how many guardians still stand.

There is no way around it. Without clearance, walking, bridging, tunnelling, ender pearls, chorus fruit, elytra,
riptide and mounts all put you back outside the sector.

## The burning city

Past the gate, a road leads down into the final sector under a red sky. Mobs here are levels 25–30
<!-- Realms.ASHEN_REACH final band -->, the highest in the realm.

- **Rest at the first shrine** beside the end of the gate road. The Citadel keeps its own respawn point: die in here
  and you wake at the last shrine you rested at inside the Citadel, or at this first one.
- The Burning City starts about 100 blocks past the gate. The route climbs north up the hill: the outskirts, a
  burning street, the market, the gatehouse in the city wall, the inner ring, then the courtyard and the keep.
- New enemies guard it: [Cinder Hounds](../../encyclopedia/mobs/ashen-reach/cinder-hound.md) in packs,
  [Sparking Marksmen](../../encyclopedia/mobs/ashen-reach/sparking-marksman.md) at range, and the heavy
  [Furnace Golem](../../encyclopedia/mobs/ashen-reach/furnace-golem.md) and
  [Smoldering Knight](../../encyclopedia/mobs/ashen-reach/smoldering-knight.md). The golem and the knight are the
  only source of the {{item embervoid:furnace_heart_cuirass}}.
- The keep's treasury is the richest chest in the realm.

Fire is everywhere. Smoldering Plate pieces cut fire damage, and the full set stops burning from hurting you at all;
Resilience helps too.

## The Smoldering Lord

The [Smoldering Lord](../../encyclopedia/bosses/smoldering-lord.md) waits in the courtyard, about 300 blocks from the
landing. He fights with the tricks of all three zone bosses, so everything you learned on the way counts. His page has
his phases and tells.

Go in rested, with a full flask, and with a party if you have one: his health and his adds grow with every fighter.

## When he falls

The first time you beat him you get the {{item embervoid:smoldering_lord_soul}} and a
{{item embervoid:flask_ember}}. Feed the ember to your flask: every sip heals more, for good. The soul, two Smoldering
Ingots and a golden sword make the {{item embervoid:royal_ash_rapier}}.

Ashen Reach is now complete for you: its obelisk in the hub turns kindled, and the next obelisk in the ring lights
up. To leave, use the Return Obelisk at the landing.
