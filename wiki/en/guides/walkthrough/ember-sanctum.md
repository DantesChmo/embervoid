The [Ember Sanctum](../../encyclopedia/places/ember-sanctum.md) is the hub: an island floating in a void under an
eternal dusk, in a dimension of its own. Every player on a server shares the same one. No monsters spawn here and
nothing on the island can be broken or built on. Mind the edge: a fall into the void kills you, and you wake at your
bed in the overworld.

Your first arrival unlocks Realm 1, Ashen Reach.

## The Ashen Maiden

The [Ashen Maiden](../../encyclopedia/npcs/ashen-maiden.md) is why you came. Right-click her to open the level-up
screen. It shows:

- your level and your seven stats,
- what each stat gives you now and what one more point would give,
- the XP points the next level costs and the XP points you have.

Click **+** next to a stat to buy one point. The XP comes out of your vanilla experience bar. Each level costs a
little more than the last; the first costs 26 XP points <!-- leveling.costBase + costLinear + costQuadratic at level 1 -->.

## Your first points

There is no wrong first point, but some are more useful early:

- **Vitality** for more health: always good.
- **Endurance** if you use a Sanctum weapon's special attack: more stamina, faster regeneration.
- **Strength** for axes and the Ashbearer's Mace, **Dexterity** for swords, bows and the Sanctum Longsword.

The stats are explained in [Stats and leveling](../mechanics/stats-and-leveling.md). There is no way to take a
point back, so spread them with your weapon in mind.

## The Ember Shrine

Right-click the [Ember Shrine](../../encyclopedia/blocks/functional/ember-shrine.md) to rest: full health, full flask.
Use a {{item embervoid:flask_shard}} on it to add a flask charge. The hub's shrine does not set your respawn point.

## The Void Obelisks

A ring of four [Void Obelisks](../../encyclopedia/blocks/functional/void-obelisk.md), one per realm. Each looks
different for each player:

- **Dark:** the realm is locked. "The flame has not reached it."
- **Lit:** right-click to travel. You arrive at the last Ember Shrine you rested at in that realm, or at its landing if
  you have not rested there yet. Sneak while you right-click to go to the landing anyway.
- **Kindled:** you have completed that realm. You can still travel there.

Only Ashen Reach exists so far.

## Leaving

The hub has its own Cleft Stele, always lit. Walk into its passage to return to the world's lit stele; you come out
beside it. If no stele is lit in the world any more, it takes you to where you last left the overworld, or to your
bed, or to the world spawn.
