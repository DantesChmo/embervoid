You have beaten the Smoldering Lord. Here is what is left to do in 0.5.3.

## What unlocks

- Your Ashen Reach obelisk in the hub is **kindled**. It still takes you there.
- The **second obelisk** lights up. Step to it and it tells you the flame burns there, but the way beyond is still
  forming. More realms are planned; this one is not open yet.
- The {{item embervoid:royal_ash_rapier}} is now within reach (see the previous page).

## Finishing your gear

The realm stays open and its zones keep spawning monsters. Things worth going back for:

- **Armor set pieces** drop from the zones' monsters, more often from the placed tanks and elites, and from the
  treasure chests. A full set gives a bonus. See [Armor sets](../mechanics/armor-sets.md).
- **Unique pieces**: the Scarecrow's Mask (Fields), Huntsman's Greaves (Woods), Drowned Sabatons (Necropolis) and the
  Furnace Heart Cuirass (Citadel). They are rare drops, likelier from each zone's elite.
- **Rings and artifacts** for the [Reliquary](../mechanics/reliquary.md): treasure chests, and now and then a zone's
  elite.
- **Ores** for Smoldering Ingots: mine them, or farm the zone monsters that drop them.

Placed enemies and opened chests do not come back. Natural spawns do.

## Rematches

A beaten boss stays beaten: you can walk through its arena in peace. To fight it again, use an
{{item embervoid:ash_of_rematch}} on the arena's Ember Shrine, then step into the arena within a minute. A rematch
gives the boss's loot and XP, but never its soul or a Flask Shard.

Ash of Rematch is crafted from 4 Cinder Shards, a Royal Amber and a bone, and turns up in the arena chests.

A boss also comes back whenever someone who has not beaten it walks into its arena. Help a friend with their fight
and you get the loot and XP too, and a chance at the boss's soul once more
<!-- boss.repeatSoulChance -->.

## The overworld

Moon nights keep coming every few weeks of game time, and the Vampire scales with your progress. See
[Moon nights](../mechanics/moon-nights.md) and [The Vampire](../mechanics/vampire.md). New Ruined Sanctums give
more Crying Souls if you want to move your stele.
