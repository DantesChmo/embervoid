Behind the fog wall at the bottom of a Ruined Sanctum waits the
[Fallen Sister](../../encyclopedia/bosses/fallen-sister.md), the first boss. She is meant to be beaten by a level 1
character in iron or diamond gear, solo, in about two minutes with dodging. Her moves and tells are on her page;
this page is about getting ready.

## Before you go in

- **Rest at the shrine by the boss door.** It fills your health and your flask.
- **Offer your Flask Shards** at that shrine first. Every shard is one more sip in the fight.
- **Bring a shield and good armor.** Iron is enough if you dodge; diamond forgives mistakes.
- **Bring your best weapon.** A Sanctum weapon from the halls is a step above iron. Keep some stamina in reserve for
  its special.
- **Put away what you cannot lose.** If you die, your items and XP go into an
  [Ash Remnant](../mechanics/death-and-remnant.md) at the spot, and you wake at your bed. You will have to walk back
  down to get them.

## The fight

Walk through the fog wall and approach the middle of the arena: she wakes, a boss bar appears and the fog wall
closes behind you. You cannot leave until the fight ends.

- Watch for the wind-up before each blow and step out of it rather than trading hits.
- When she drops below half health, the second phase begins and new attacks come from more than one direction.
  Keep moving.
- Sip from your flask when there is a gap, not in the middle of her combo: a sip takes a moment and you cannot drink
  while sprinting.

If you die or leave, the fight ends and she is back at full health for the next attempt. Your remnant lies in the
arena, so the next attempt starts with taking it back: right-click it as soon as you are in.

## The reward

When she falls, everyone who fought her and is still inside the arena gets a {{item embervoid:crying_soul}} straight
into their inventory. Your first clear of any Sanctum also gives a {{item embervoid:flask_shard}}, a Sanctum weapon and
some supplies.

Each Sanctum's boss falls once per world and does not return. If you need another Crying Soul (because a friend needs
one, or you want to move your stele), clear another Ruined Sanctum.

At the far end of the arena stands a cold Cleft Stele. Your soul kindles it.
