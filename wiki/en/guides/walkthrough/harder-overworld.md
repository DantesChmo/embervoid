The moment Embervoid is installed, the night gets more dangerous. Nothing new is added to the day; what changes is
what hunts you after dark and underground.

## Every monster has a level

Each hostile mob gets a level when it spawns. Look at one and its name shows "Lv. N". Higher levels mean more health,
more damage and more XP when it dies. Levels climb the further you go from the world spawn, below y = 0, in the
Nether and in the End. A zombie near spawn is level 3 <!-- MobConfig: minecraft:zombie base level -->; an enderman
starts at 12 <!-- MobConfig: minecraft:enderman base level -->.

Mobs also hit harder on every difficulty. Easy now hits like vanilla Normal; Hard hits harder than vanilla Hard. The
details are in [Mob levels and difficulty](../mechanics/mob-levels.md).

What to do about it:

- Craft a shield and armor early, and do not wander far from spawn at night in leather.
- Treat caves below y = 0 as a step up: mobs there are a few levels higher.
- Light your caves. Light is also the answer to one of the new hostiles.

## Three new hostiles

- [Ash Ghoul](../../encyclopedia/mobs/overworld/ash-ghoul.md): fast, comes in packs at night and in dark caves. When
  one finds you, the others nearby join in. Killing one leaves an ash cloud that blinds you: back off before you kill
  the next. They burn in daylight.
- [Hollow](../../encyclopedia/mobs/overworld/hollow.md): a slow, armored swordsman on the surface at night, rare. It
  raises a guard that blocks blows and arrows from the front. Hit it with an **axe** to break the guard and stagger it,
  then strike. Watch for the wind-up before its swing.
- [Ember Wisp](../../encyclopedia/mobs/overworld/ember-wisp.md): a small flyer deep underground in total darkness. It
  dives at you and sets things on fire. Bright light drives it off, so place torches and it flees.

## Why XP matters now

Vanilla experience is Embervoid's currency. You spend it on stats with the Ashen Maiden, one point at a time, once
you reach the hub. So keep the XP you earn: every mob, every ore, every furnace load counts. Enchanting is still
there, but every level you spend on it is a level you do not put into your character.

If you die, your XP is not lost: it waits in your [Ash Remnant](../mechanics/death-and-remnant.md) with your items.
Go back and right-click it to take everything back.

You also start with a {{item embervoid:healing_flask}}. Drink it instead of eating in a fight; resting refills it. See
[Healing Flask](../mechanics/healing-flask.md).
