A [Cleft Stele](../../encyclopedia/blocks/functional/cleft-stele.md) is a ritual circle of Cleft Stone. Kindled with a
{{item embervoid:crying_soul}}, it opens a passage to the Ember Sanctum, the hub.

## The quick way: the dungeon's stele

Every Ruined Sanctum arena has a cold stele on its dais. Right-click any of its stones with the Crying Soul. The soul
is used up, the pillars flare and a column of mist rises over the centre stone. Walk into the mist, or right-click the
stele, to travel to the hub.

The downside: the stele stays down in the dungeon, and you have to walk there every time.

## Building your own

You can build a stele anywhere in the Overworld, the Nether or the End, for example next to your base.

1. Craft Cleft Stone: 8 stone bricks around 1 coal or charcoal makes 8 <!-- recipe embervoid:cleft_stone -->.
   A circle takes 13, so craft twice.
2. Place one stone as the **centre**.
3. Build four **pillars**, each 3 stones tall, standing on the same level as the centre stone, two blocks out from it
   to the north, east, south and west. That leaves one empty block between the centre and each pillar.
4. Keep the 3 blocks above the centre stone clear: that is where the passage rises.
5. Right-click any stone of the circle with a Crying Soul.

The soul's tooltip repeats the shape. A circle will not kindle in a realm or in the hub; the soul is kept if you try.

Steles built as a flat slab before 0.5.3 still work.

## One stele per world

A world has only one lit stele at a time (the hub's own does not count). Kindling a new one puts the old one out, for
everybody on the server; the players who used the old one are told. Breaking any stone of a lit stele also puts it out.

With no lit stele, the hub's way out takes you back to where you last left the overworld, or else to your bed or the
world spawn. You never get stuck in the hub.

So when you move your stele, you spend a new Crying Soul, and you need a new Ruined Sanctum for it.
