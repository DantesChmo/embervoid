# Credits

## Sounds

Every sound effect in Embervoid is a vanilla Minecraft sound file (© Mojang Studios), played through Embervoid's
own sound events with their own pitch, volume and variant choice (`assets/embervoid/sounds.json`). The mod ships no
audio files of its own for effects: the game supplies them.
